# Role: Business Intelligence Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Analytics Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Business Intelligence (BI)


## Purpose
To manage the implementation and maintenance of corporate reporting and visualization.


## Responsibilities
- Supervise a team of BI Analysts.
- Oversee the design and development of complex dashboards.
- Manage the "Self-service" reporting environment and **Personnel** training.
- Ensure data visualizations are accurate, clear, and actionable.
- Partner with branch leads to define reporting requirements.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Reporting Suites**** | Branch-specific suites for operational visibility. |
| ****Personnel Training Guides**** | Guides for navigating corporate BI tools. |
| ****Quality Audits**** | Regular audits of dashboard accuracy and design. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Business Intelligence (BI)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Viz Design**** | Final say on the visual layout of dashboards. |
| ****Metric Mapping**** | Responsibility for mapping business logic to data. |
