# Role: VP of Data Science
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Analytics Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Analytics (CDO)


## Purpose
To manage the advanced analytics and machine learning functions. The VP of Data Science focuses on **"Predictive Insights"** and leveraging **Agentic Firmware** to improve **Sesha Corporate OS**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Data Science and Machine Learning teams.
- Oversee the development of predictive models and algorithms.
- Drive the innovation of data-driven features in the platform.
- Ensure the quality and ethics of **Agentic and ML models**.
- Collaborate with Product and Engineering on ML integration.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for data science and **Agentic research**. |
| ****Performance Reports**** | Reports on model accuracy and operational impact. |
| ****Analytics Whitepapers**** | Thought leadership on predictive methodologies. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Analytics (CDO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Model Deployment**** | Final sign-off on promoting models to production. |
| ****Research Scope**** | Authority over priorities of research projects. |
| ****Algorithmic Standards**** | Final say on methodology for predictive modeling. |
