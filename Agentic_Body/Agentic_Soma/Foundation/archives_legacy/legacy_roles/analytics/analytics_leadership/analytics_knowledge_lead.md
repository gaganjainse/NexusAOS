# Role: Analytics Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Analytics Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Analytics (CDO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Data Intelligence & KPI Brain"** of the Analytics Branch. The Analytics Knowledge Lead ensures that KPI definitions, data schemas, and insight reports are structured for **Agentic Data Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing data "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Corporate KPI Dictionary and Data Catalog.
- Maintain the **"Data Lineage & Logic Knowledge Graph."**


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****KPI Dictionary**** | Firmware-ready dictionary of all corporate metrics. |
| ****Logic Repository**** | Repository for data schemas and transformation logic. |
| ****Discovery Logs**** | Logs of identified operational and business insights. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Analytics (CDO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Metric Definitions**** | Final sign-off on corporate KPI calculation logic. |
| ****Data Catalog**** | Authority over the structure of data metadata. |
