# Role: HR Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"People & Culture Brain"** of the HR Branch. The HR Knowledge Lead ensures that policies, hiring standards, and culture artifacts are machine-readable and up-to-date.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing culture "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the **Personnel Handbook** and Corporate Policy repository.
- Maintain the **"Talent Knowledge Graph"** (Competency models, role definitions).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Policy Repository**** | Firmware-ready repository of all HR policies. |
| ****Culture Framework**** | Structured framework for values and corporate culture. |
| ****Competency Artifacts**** | Artifacts defining required role skills and competencies. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Policy Language**** | Final sign-off on **Agentic** wording of policies. |
| ****Competency Models**** | Authority over the structure of role requirements. |
