# Role: Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global people strategy, culture, and talent management for **Sesha Corporate OS**. The CHRO ensures the corporation has the right talent to achieve its mission.


## Responsibilities
- Define the annual people strategy and HR budget.
- Oversee corporate culture, values, and **Personnel** engagement.
- Lead the HR leadership team (Recruiting, Payroll, Culture).
- Manage high-level **Personnel** relations and organizational design.
- Represent the HR branch in HQ-level strategy sessions.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****People Strategy**** | Annual strategic plan for talent and culture. |
| ****Culture Manifesto**** | Formal definition of corporate values and culture. |
| ****Health Reports**** | Regular reports on organizational morale and health. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****HR Spend**** | Final approval on all HR and training budgets. |
| ****Org Design**** | Authority over organization-wide structural changes. |
| ****Exec Recruiting**** | Approval of Director-level and above hires. |
