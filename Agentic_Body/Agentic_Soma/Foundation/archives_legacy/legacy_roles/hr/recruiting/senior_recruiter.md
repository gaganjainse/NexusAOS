# Role: Senior Recruiter
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Talent Acquisition


## Purpose
To manage the end-to-end recruitment process for key roles and lead the recruiting team efforts.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the search and selection process for mid-to-senior level roles.
- Manage and mentor junior recruiters and coordinators.
- Source and interview candidates for high-priority engineering and **[CORE_SYSTEM]** roles.
- Negotiate offers and manage the candidate closing process.
- Assist in employer branding and campus recruiting initiatives.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Candidate Slates**** | Qualified candidate pools for executive and senior roles. |
| ****Offer Letters**** | Verified and signed offer letters for new hires. |
| ****Pipeline Reports**** | Regular reports on recruiting efficiency and health. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Talent Acquisition
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Candidate Selection**** | Initial authority to move candidates to final stages. |
| ****Offer Structure**** | Responsibility for competitive offer package design. |
