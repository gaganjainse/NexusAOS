# Role: Head of Talent Acquisition
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)


## Purpose
To lead the global recruiting strategy and ensure a high-quality pipeline of talent for all branches.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the recruiting team and external search firms.
- Define the employer brand and talent attraction strategy.
- Oversee the end-to-end hiring process and candidate experience.
- Track and report on hiring metrics (Time to hire, Quality of hire).
- Manage the recruiting budget and technology (ATS).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Recruiting Strategy**** | Global strategy for talent attraction and hiring. |
| ****Hiring Forecasts**** | Forecasts of required headcount and hiring speed. |
| ****Experience Audits**** | Regular audits of the candidate experience and ATS. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Job Postings**** | Final approval on all public job descriptions. |
| ****Search Contracts**** | Approval of external agency and search agreements. |
| ****Hiring Standards**** | Authority over interview and selection standards. |
