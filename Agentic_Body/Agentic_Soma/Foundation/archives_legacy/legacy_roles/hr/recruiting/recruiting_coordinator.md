# Role: Recruiting Coordinator
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Senior Recruiter


## Purpose
To handle the logistical and administrative details of the recruiting process.


## Responsibilities
- Schedule interviews and manage recruiter calendars.
- Coordinate candidate travel and expense reimbursements.
- Manage the background check and reference check process.
- Assist in the preparation of offer letters and onboarding kits.
- Maintain recruiting files and documentation.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Interview Calendars**** | Master schedules for all candidate interactions. |
| ****Expense Reports**** | Verified reports for candidate travel and hiring. |
| ****Compliance Logs**** | Records of background and reference check completion. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Senior Recruiter
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over coordination of interview timings. |
| ****Logistics Accuracy**** | Responsibility for precision of travel arrangements. |
