# Role: HR Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** HR Manager (Personnel Relations)


## Purpose
To learn professional Human Resources practices while supporting the HR team.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in the organization of **Personnel** files and HR records.
- Support the recruiting team in scheduling and sourcing.
- Help organize **Personnel** engagement and culture events.
- Assist in basic HR reporting and data entry.
- Conduct research on HR best practices and sector compliance.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Record Folders**** | Organized folders for **Personnel** and HR records. |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Summaries of HR best practices and compliance research. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on an HR-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** HR Manager (Personnel Relations)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by an HR Professional. |
