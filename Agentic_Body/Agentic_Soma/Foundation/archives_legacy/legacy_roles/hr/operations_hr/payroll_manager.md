# Role: Payroll & Benefits Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of People Operations


## Purpose
To ensure accurate and timely payroll processing and administration of **Personnel** benefits.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the monthly/bi-weekly payroll cycle.
- Oversee the administration of health, retirement, and other benefits.
- Ensure payroll sector compliance with local and national tax laws.
- Handle **Personnel** inquiries regarding pay and benefits.
- Manage relationships with payroll and benefits vendors.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Payroll Runs**** | Verified and finalized corporate payroll cycles. |
| ****Benefits Reports**** | Reports on **Personnel** participation in benefits programs. |
| ****Compliance Records**** | Documentation of payroll tax and legal compliance. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of People Operations
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Pay Adjustments**** | Authority to approve salary changes and bonuses. |
| ****Vendor Coordination**** | Responsibility for daily interaction with providers. |
