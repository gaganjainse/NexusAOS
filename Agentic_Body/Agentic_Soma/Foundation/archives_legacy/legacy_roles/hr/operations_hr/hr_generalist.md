# Role: HR Generalist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** HR Manager (Personnel Relations)


## Purpose
To provide broad administrative and operational support across the HR function.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in **Personnel** onboarding and offboarding.
- Update and maintain the HRIS (**Personnel** Information System).
- Manage **Personnel** leave and attendance records.
- Support **Personnel** engagement events and internal communications.
- Assist in drafting and updating HR policies.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****HRIS Records**** | Live and accurate updates to **Personnel** data systems. |
| ****Attendance Reports**** | Verified records of **Personnel** leave and attendance. |
| ****Internal Comms**** | Drafts of internal HR announcements and updates. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** HR Manager (Personnel Relations)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Data Integrity**** | Responsibility for accuracy of assigned HRIS data. |
| ****Doc Preparation**** | Authority over formatting of internal HR documents. |
