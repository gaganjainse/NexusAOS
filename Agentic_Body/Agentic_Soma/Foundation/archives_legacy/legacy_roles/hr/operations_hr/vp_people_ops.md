# Role: VP of People Operations
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)


## Purpose
To manage the operational functions of HR, including payroll, benefits, sector compliance, and **Personnel relations**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the People Ops team and **Agentic systems** (HRIS).
- Oversee payroll and benefits administration.
- Ensure corporate-wide HR sector compliance and policy management.
- Manage **Personnel** performance review processes.
- Lead the "Culture and Engagement" initiatives.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****HR Roadmap**** | Operational roadmap for HR systems and services. |
| ****Audit Reports**** | Reports on benefits and payroll compliance. |
| ****Engagement Results**** | Outcomes of corporate culture and morale surveys. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****HR Policy**** | Final say on **Personnel Handbook** content. |
| ****Benefits Selection**** | Approval of health and wellness providers. |
| ****Personnel Relations**** | Authority over standard **Personnel** dispute resolution. |
