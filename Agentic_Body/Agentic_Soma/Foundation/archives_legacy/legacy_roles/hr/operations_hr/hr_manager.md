# Role: HR Manager (Personnel Relations)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of People Operations


## Purpose
To manage day-to-day **Personnel relations**, performance management, and HR policy execution.


## Responsibilities
- Act as the primary point of contact for **Personnel** HR inquiries.
- Support managers in performance improvement and disciplinary actions.
- Conduct onboarding and offboarding processes.
- Manage the local HR records and documentation.
- Assist in corporate culture and engagement events.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Onboarding Kits**** | Complete checklists and kits for new hire success. |
| ****Incident Records**** | Documentation of **Personnel** relations and performance. |
| ****Review Summaries**** | Summaries of **Personnel** performance review outcomes. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of People Operations
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Record Accuracy**** | Responsibility for the integrity of **Personnel** files. |
| ****Onboarding Timing**** | Authority over the schedule of new hire orientations. |
