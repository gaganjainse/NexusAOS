# Role: Marketing Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Marketing Branch leadership and teams.


## Responsibilities
- Schedule marketing coordination and campaign reviews.
- Manage marketing vendor invoices and agency contracts.
- Coordinate marketing department travel and event logistics.
- Assist in the organization of brand workshops and creative sessions.
- Maintain the marketing branch internal wiki and asset logs.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for marketing leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department and agency spend. |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Records of campaign strategy and creative syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over marketing leadership availability. |
| ****Asset Access**** | Initial review of team-level asset and tool requests. |
