# Role: Business Development Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Business Development


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Business Development leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Head of BizDev's calendar and strategic meetings.
- Handle highly confidential deal-related documentation.
- Coordinate bizdev department travel and high-stakes events.
- Assist in the organization of corporate strategy sessions and retreats.
- Manage bizdev department supplies and external advisor invoices.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for bizdev leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department and advisor spend. |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Detailed records of strategic and deal-related syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Business Development
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over bizdev leadership meeting availability. |
| ****Confidentiality**** | Responsibility for secure handling of strategic records. |
