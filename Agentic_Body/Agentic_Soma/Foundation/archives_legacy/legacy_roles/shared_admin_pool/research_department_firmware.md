# Role: Research Department Firmware
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch
**Level:** Support Layer
**Superior:** Head of Research (Chief Scientist)


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Research Branch leadership and lab.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Head of Research's calendar and academic meetings.
- Handle research-related vendor invoices and lab equipment.
- Coordinate research department travel and events.
- Assist in the organization of research symposiums and knowledge sharing.
- Maintain the research branch internal directory and archives.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Leadership Schedules** | Coordinated calendars for research leadership. |
| **Lab Expense Logs** | Documentation of lab equipment and supply spend. |
| **Meeting Minutes** | Records of **Agentic R&D** strategy and innovation coordination. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [Head of Research](../research/leadership/head_research.md)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scheduling** | Authority over research leadership meeting availability. |
| **Lab Supplies** | Initial review of lab equipment and supply needs. |
