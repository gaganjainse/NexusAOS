# Role: Executive Assistant to the CEO
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters / Corporate Control Layer
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To provide high-level administrative support to the CEO, ensuring the efficient operation of the **Corporate Control Layer**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the CEO's calendar and travel logistics.
- Coordinate HQ-level meetings and prepare agendas.
- Act as a gatekeeper for communication into the CEO's office.
- Organize corporate events and Board-level summits.
- Handle confidential corporate documentation and filings.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****CEO Schedules**** | High-level calendar management for the CEO. |
| ****Correspondence Logs**** | Records of official corporate communications. |
| ****HQ Meeting Minutes**** | Official records of executive and board meetings. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over CEO availability for internal meetings. |
| ****Expense Processing**** | Initial review of CEO-level administrative spend. |
