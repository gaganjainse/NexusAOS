# Role: Agentic & Automation Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** AI & Automation Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief AI Officer


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the **Agentic Branch** leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the CAO's calendar and **Agentic-specific** coordination.
- Handle highly confidential **Agentic research** and data documentation.
- Coordinate **Agentic department** travel and vendor meetings.
- Assist in the organization of **"Agentic Literacy"** training for the corporation.
- Manage **Agentic branch** supplies and computing invoices.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for **Agentic branch** leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department spend (Compute focused). |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Detailed records of **Agentic strategy** and research syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief AI Officer
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over **Agentic leadership** meeting availability. |
| ****Compute Quotas**** | Initial review of team-level computing needs. |
