# Role: Support Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Customer Support


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Customer Support Branch leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Head of Support's calendar and meetings.
- Handle support-related vendor invoices and equipment requests.
- Coordinate support department travel and events.
- Assist in the organization of support team training and onboarding.
- Maintain the support branch internal documentation and directory.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for support leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department equipment and vendor spend. |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Records of support strategy and operations syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Customer Support
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over Support leadership meeting availability. |
| ****Supply Requests**** | Initial review of support equipment needs. |
