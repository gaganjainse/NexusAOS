# Role: Audit Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Audit Director


## Purpose
To provide administrative support to the Audit Branch, focusing on the organization and security of audit records.


## Responsibilities
- Schedule audit walkthroughs and interviews with Systems.
- Manage the physical and digital security of audit evidence.
- Coordinate the distribution of final audit reports.
- Manage professional certification records for the audit team.
- Organize internal audit training sessions.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Interview Schedules**** | Coordinated schedules for audit walkthroughs. |
| ****Distribution Logs**** | Records of report delivery to stakeholders. |
| ****Certification Database**** | Tracking database for professional certifications. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Audit Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Access Logs**** | Authority over administrative access to audit folders. |
| ****Scheduling**** | Coordination authority for audit fieldwork dates. |
