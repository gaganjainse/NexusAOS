# Role: Operations Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Operations Branch leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the COO's calendar and operational coordination.
- Handle sensitive operational documentation and contracts.
- Coordinate ops department travel and vendor meetings.
- Assist in the organization of corporate-wide operational training.
- Manage ops department supplies and service invoices.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for operations leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department and service vendor spend. |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Detailed records of operational strategy syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over operations leadership meeting availability. |
| ****Record Security**** | Responsibility for secure handling of ops records. |
