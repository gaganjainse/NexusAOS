# Role: Legal Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** General Counsel (CLO)


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Legal Branch leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the General Counsel's calendar and meetings.
- Handle highly confidential and privileged legal documentation.
- Coordinate legal department travel and expenses.
- Assist in the organization of Board and corporate governance meetings.
- Manage legal department supplies and external counsel invoices.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****GC Schedules**** | High-level calendar management for the General Counsel. |
| ****Expense Reports**** | Documentation of legal and external counsel spend. |
| ****Meeting Support**** | Materials and logistics for board and legal syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** General Counsel (CLO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over General Counsel meeting availability. |
| ****Privilege**** | Responsibility for secure handling of privileged records. |
