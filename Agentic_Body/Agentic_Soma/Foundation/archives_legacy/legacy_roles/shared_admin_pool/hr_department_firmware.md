# Role: Hr Department Firmware
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Support Layer
**Superior:** Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)


## Purpose
To provide administrative support to the HR leadership and manage department-level logistics.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the HR leadership team's calendars and meetings.
- Handle sensitive and confidential HR documentation.
- Coordinate HR department travel and expenses.
- Assist in the organization of corporate-wide HR events.
- Manage HR department supplies and vendor invoices.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Leadership Schedules** | Coordinated calendars for HR branch leadership. |
| **Expense Logs** | Documentation of department event and vendor spend. |
| **Meeting Minutes** | Records of HR strategy and **People Operations** coordination. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [VP of People Ops](../hr/operations/vp_people_ops.md)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scheduling** | Authority over HR leadership meeting availability. |
| **Confidentiality** | Responsibility for secure handling of sensitive HR records. |
