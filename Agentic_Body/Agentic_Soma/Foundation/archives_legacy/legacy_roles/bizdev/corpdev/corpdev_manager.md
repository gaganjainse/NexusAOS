# Role: Corporate Development Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Corporate Development (M&A)


## Purpose
To manage the **Agentic** and operational details of M&A and investment deals.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the financial modeling and valuation for potential deals.
- Coordinate the due diligence process across departments (Legal, Systems, HR).
- Assist in the drafting of deal-related documents and presentations.
- Manage the deal pipeline and tracking system.
- Support the integration of acquired companies.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Valuation Models**** | Finalized financial models for deal valuation. |
| ****Due Diligence Summaries**** | Summaries of findings from the due diligence process. |
| ****Deal Tracking Logs**** | Live logs tracking the progress of active deals. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Corporate Development (M&A)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Model Accuracy**** | Responsibility for the financial correctness of models. |
| ****DD Coordination**** | Authority over the scheduling of due diligence. |
