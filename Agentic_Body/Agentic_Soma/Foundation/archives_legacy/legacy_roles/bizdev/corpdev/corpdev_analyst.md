# Role: Corporate Development Analyst
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Corporate Development Manager


## Purpose
To support the M&A and investment process through detailed financial and operational analysis.


## Responsibilities
- Support financial modeling and valuation for potential deals.
- Assist in the data gathering and organization for due diligence.
- Conduct research on potential M&A **Entities** and investment areas.
- Update and maintain the corporate deal pipeline.
- Assist in the preparation of investment memorandums.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Deal Analysis Packages**** | Financial and operational analysis of potential deals. |
| ****Entity Profiles**** | Detailed profile sheets on target **Entities**. |
| ****Due Diligence Files**** | Organized data files for the due diligence process. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Corporate Development Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Model Integrity**** | Responsibility for accuracy of model inputs. |
| ****Data Organization**** | Authority over the structure of deal files. |
