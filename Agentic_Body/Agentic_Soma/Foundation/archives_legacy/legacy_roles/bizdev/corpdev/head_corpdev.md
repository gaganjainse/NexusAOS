# Role: Head of Corporate Development (M&A)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Business Development


## Purpose
To lead the corporation's inorganic growth strategy, including mergers, acquisitions, and strategic investments.


## Responsibilities
- Define the M&A and investment strategy.
- Identify and evaluate potential acquisition **Entities** and investment opportunities.
- Oversee the deal execution process (Due Diligence, Valuation, Negotiation).
- Manage relationships with investment banks and corporate venture partners.
- Coordinate post-merger integration (PMI) strategy.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****M&A Pipeline Report**** | Regular reporting on the global M&A deal pipeline. |
| ****Investment Memos**** | Strategic memorandums for deal approval. |
| ****Integration Plans**** | Strategic plans for post-merger integration. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Business Development
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Entity Selection**** | Authority over criteria for potential M&A targets. |
| ****Valuation Logic**** | Final say on financial valuation methodology. |
| ****Deal Execution**** | Initial authority to proceed with formal due diligence. |
