# Role: Strategy Analyst
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Strategy Manager


## Purpose
To provide detailed research and analysis to support corporate strategic initiatives.


## Responsibilities
- Conduct market sizing and trend analysis.
- Build and maintain corporate strategic and financial models.
- Assist in the preparation of strategic presentations and memos.
- Support data gathering for special strategic projects.
- Monitor corporate KPI performance against strategic targets.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Packages**** | Comprehensive market research data packages. |
| ****Analysis Summaries**** | Summaries of strategic analysis findings. |
| ****Strategy Models**** | Updated and maintained corporate strategy models. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Strategy Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Analysis Logic**** | Responsibility for the accuracy of assigned analysis. |
| ****Visual Presentation**** | Authority over the layout of assigned charts. |
