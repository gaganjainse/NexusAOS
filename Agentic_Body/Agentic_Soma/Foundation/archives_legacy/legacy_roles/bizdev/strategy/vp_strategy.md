# Role: VP of Strategy
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Business Development


## Purpose
To manage the corporate strategy and market intelligence functions. The VP of Strategy focuses on "Where we compete and how we win".


## Responsibilities
- Manage the corporate strategy and market intelligence teams.
- Oversee the corporate strategic planning cycle.
- Conduct competitive intelligence and market trend analysis.
- Lead special projects for the CEO and Head of BizDev.
- Coordinate strategic initiatives across other branches.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Strategic Plan**** | Annual corporate strategy and execution plan. |
| ****Intel Reports**** | Quarterly competitive intelligence summaries. |
| ****Trend Packages**** | Analysis packages on market and industry trends. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Business Development
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Strategic Models**** | Final say on structure of corporate models. |
| ****Project Selection**** | Authority over strategic research priorities. |
| ****Intel Reports**** | Final approval of global market briefings. |
