# Role: BizDev Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Business Development


## Purpose
To manage the **"Strategy & Market Entry Brain"** of the BizDev Branch. The BizDev Knowledge Lead ensures that expansion roadmaps, M&A data, and strategic playbooks are structured for **Agentic Strategy Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing strategy "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Strategic Playbook and Market Entry repository.
- Maintain the **"Strategic Opportunity Knowledge Graph."**

| ****Knowledge Base**** | Repository for market entry and expansion data. |
