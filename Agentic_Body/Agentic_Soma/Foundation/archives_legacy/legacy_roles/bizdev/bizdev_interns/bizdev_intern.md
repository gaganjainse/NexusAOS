# Role: Business Development Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Strategy Manager


## Purpose
To learn corporate strategy and development practices while supporting the **BizDev team**.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in market research and competitor monitoring.
- Support the organization of data for strategic projects and M&A.
- Help prepare presentations and memos for corporate leadership.
- Conduct research on potential strategic partners or **Entities**.
- Complete a business-focused internship project.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Briefs**** | Market and competitor research summaries. |
| ****Strategic Data**** | Organized data files for strategic initiatives. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a business-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Strategy Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by a BizDev Professional. |
