# Role: Support Specialist (Tier 1)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Support Supervisor


## Purpose
To provide immediate, high-quality assistance to **Sesha Corporate OS** **Sovereigns** via **Oracle Interface**, email, and phone.


## Responsibilities
- Respond to **Sovereign** inquiries and issues within established SLAs.
- Troubleshoot common product questions and configuration issues.
- Document **Sovereign** interactions and issues in the support system.
- Escalate complex issues to Tier 2 according to established protocols.
- Assist in maintaining the public-facing knowledge base.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Resolved Tickets**** | **Sovereign** inquiries resolved at the Tier 1 level. |
| ****Ticket Logs**** | Accurate and detailed records of **Sovereign** interactions. |
| ****Draft Articles**** | Preliminary drafts for knowledge base updates. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Support Supervisor
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Response Content**** | Authority over tone and clarity of **Sovereign** responses. |
| ****Escalation Readiness**** | Responsibility for gathering info before Tier 2 handoff. |
