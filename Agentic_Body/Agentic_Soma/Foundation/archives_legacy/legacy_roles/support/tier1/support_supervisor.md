# Role: Support Supervisor
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Support Operations Manager


## Purpose
To provide direct supervision and guidance to the Tier 1 support **Personnel**.


## Responsibilities
- Supervise the daily activity of Support Specialists.
- Conduct real-time monitoring of ticket queues and **Oracle Interfaces**.
- Provide coaching and support for difficult **Sovereign** interactions.
- Assist in the recruitment and onboarding of new support **Personnel**.
- Perform initial quality reviews of support tickets.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Shift Schedules**** | Coordinated team shift assignments and coverage. |
| ****Coaching Logs**** | Weekly records of **Personnel** performance coaching sessions. |
| ****Queue Status**** | Real-time status updates on support queue health. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Support Operations Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Shift Management**** | Authority over **Personnel** scheduling and break coverage. |
| ****Refund Initiation**** | Initial approval for standard **Sovereign** refund requests. |
