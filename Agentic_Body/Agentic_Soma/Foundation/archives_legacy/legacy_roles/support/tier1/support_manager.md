# Role: Support Operations Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Customer Support


## Purpose
To manage the operational functions of the support team and ensure efficient ticket resolution.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Tier 1 support team and daily operations.
- Monitor support metrics and ensure SLAs are met.
- Oversee support **Personnel** training and quality assurance.
- Manage the Helpdesk software and support workflows.
- Coordinate the creation of internal and external help documentation.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Metrics Dashboard**** | Weekly dashboard tracking key support SLAs. |
| ****Performance Reviews**** | Regular assessments of support **Personnel** performance. |
| ****QA Audit Summaries**** | Summaries of support ticket and interaction audits. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Customer Support
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Workflow Changes**** | Authority to approve changes to ticket routing logic. |
| ****Tooling Access**** | Approval of **Personnel** permissions within support software. |
| ****Standard Responses**** | Final sign-off on support macros and templates. |
