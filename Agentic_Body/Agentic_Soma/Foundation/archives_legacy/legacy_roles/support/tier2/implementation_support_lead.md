# Role: Implementation Support Lead (Tier 2/3)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Customer Support


## Purpose
To manage high-level **Agentic escalations** and ensure deep product expertise within the support branch.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Tier 2 and Tier 3 **Agentic support** implementers.
- Oversee the investigation of complex bugs and **Agentic issues**.
- Coordinate with Deployment for **Firmware Asset** fixes and patches.
- Lead **Agentic training** for the wider support team.
- Manage the **Agentic support laboratory** and test environments.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Escalation Reports**** | Status reports on high-level **Agentic escalations**. |
| ****RCA Documents**** | Root Cause Analysis documents for critical incidents. |
| ****Training Modules**** | **Agentic support** modules for the support team. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Customer Support
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Escalations**** | Final say on escalating issues to Engineering. |
| ****Fix Verification**** | Authority to sign-off on fixes before communication. |
| ****Advanced Knowledge**** | Final approval on advanced support articles. |
