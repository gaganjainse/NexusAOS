# Role: Findings & Reporting Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Audit Director


## Purpose
To translate raw audit evidence into clear, actionable, and professional audit reports. The Reporting Lead ensures that **"The message"** is consistent and drives remediation.


## Responsibilities
- Consolidate findings from **Agentic and Quality audits**.
- Draft the Executive Summary for the CAE and Board.
- Track the lifecycle of audit "Issues" from identification to closure.
- Manage the Audit Reporting Dashboard.
- Ensure all audit workpapers meet professional standards.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Final Audit Reports**** | Professional reports summarizing audit engagements. |
| ****Issue Dashboards**** | Live dashboards tracking finding statuses. |
| ****Management Letters**** | Official letters communicating results to leadership. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Audit Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Report Language**** | Final say on wording and tone of audit findings. |
| ****Issue Closure**** | Joint approval of "Verified Fixed" issue status. |
| ****Workpaper Quality**** | Authority to reject insufficient audit evidence. |
