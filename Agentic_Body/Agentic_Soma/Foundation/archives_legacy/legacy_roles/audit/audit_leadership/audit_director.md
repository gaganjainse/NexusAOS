# Role: Audit Director
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Audit Executive (CAE)


## Purpose
To manage the day-to-day operations of the Audit branch. The Audit Director ensures that audits are executed according to the annual plan and meet the highest standards of professional practice.


## Responsibilities
- Manage Audit Managers and Leads across all audit domains.
- Oversee the execution of the Audit Life Cycle.
- Ensure audit resources are properly allocated to high-risk areas.
- Review and approve draft audit reports before they reach the CAE.
- Manage relationships with audited departments (Systems, HQ).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Performance Summaries**** | Quarterly summaries of audit branch performance. |
| ****Engagement Reports**** | Final approved reports for specific audit engagements. |
| ****Allocation Plans**** | Resource allocation plans for the audit branch. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Audit Executive (CAE)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Audit Reports**** | Intermediate sign-off on all audit findings. |
| ****Audit Methodology**** | Approval of specific audit procedures and tools. |
| ****Remediation Plans**** | Approval of corrective actions proposed by branches. |
