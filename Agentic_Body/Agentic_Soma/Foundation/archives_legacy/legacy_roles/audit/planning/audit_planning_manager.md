# Role: Audit Planning Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Audit Director


## Purpose
To define the scope, timeline, and resource requirements for specific audit engagements. The Planning Manager ensures that audits are well-organized and targeted at the most critical risks.


## Responsibilities
- Conduct risk assessments to inform audit planning.
- Define the objectives and scope of individual audit projects.
- Set timelines and milestones for audit execution.
- Coordinate with Systems branch leads to schedule audit fieldwork.
- Maintain the **"Audit Universe"** (list of all auditable entities/processes).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Project Charters**** | Charters defining the scope and goals of audits. |
| ****Planning Memos**** | Memorandums detailing engagement strategy and risks. |
| ****Risk Worksheets**** | Structured worksheets for audit risk scoring. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Audit Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Audit Scope**** | Final say on audit boundaries for specific projects. |
| ****Risk Ratings**** | Authority over initial risk scoring of processes. |
| ****Milestones**** | Approval to transition from planning to fieldwork. |
