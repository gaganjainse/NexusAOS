# Role: Chief Agentic Officer (CAO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Agentic & Automation Branch (Head) / HQ
**Level:** Branch Head / HQ Executive
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the corporate-wide Agentic strategy and automation roadmap. The CAO ensures that Sesha OS is **"Agentic-First,"** leveraging agentic workflows and machine learning to optimize every corporate function.


## Responsibilities
- Define the global **Agentic Governance** and Ethics framework.
- Oversee the deployment of **Agentic Firmware** across all 12 corporate branches.
- Lead the Agentic & Automation Branch leadership team.
- Advise the CEO and Board on **Agentic capabilities** and competitive advantages.
- Manage the corporate "Knowledge Graph" and training data pipelines.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agentic Strategy & Roadmap** | Global strategy and roadmap for **Agentic automation**. |
| **Firmware Deployment Manifest** | Record of all **Agentic Firmware** deployed across branches. |
| **ROI Reports** | Monthly reports on automation efficiency and ROI. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [CEO](../../hq/executive/ceo.md)
- **Supervises:** [VP of Agentic Workflows](../workflows/vp_agentic_workflows.md), [Head of Model Governance](../governance/head_model_governance.md)


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Model Deployment** | Final sign-off on promoting models to production. |
| **Agentic Tooling** | Approval of all 3rd-party LLM providers and frameworks. |
| **Data Access for Agentic** | Final authority on dataset usage for model training. |
