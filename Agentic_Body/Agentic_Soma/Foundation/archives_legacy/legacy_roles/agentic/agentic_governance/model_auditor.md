# Role: Model Auditor
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Agentic & Automation Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor (Implementation)
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To perform implementation audits and bias testing on **Agentic models** and **Firmware** behaviors to ensure operational safety and ethical alignment.


## Responsibilities
- Execute safety test suites against **Agentic models** and **Strategic Inputs**.
- Monitor Firmware decision logs for "Model Drift" or bias.
- Document safety violations and escalate to the Head of Model Governance.
- Verify that models comply with industry data privacy rules.
- Support the validation of model remediations.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Model Safety Test Results** | Results from safety testing of **Agentic models**. |
| **Bias & Drift Reports** | Documentation of bias and drift in model performance. |
| **Compliance Verification** | Packages verifying sector compliance. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [Head of Model Governance](head_model_governance.md)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Audit Accuracy** | Responsibility for factual correctness of test results. |
| **Test Logic** | Authority over implementation design of safety tests. |
