# Role: Procurement Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Finance (FP&A)


## Purpose
To manage the end-to-end acquisition of goods and services, ensuring cost-efficiency, vendor reliability, and alignment with corporate budgetary goals.


## Responsibilities
- Source and evaluate vendors based on quality, price, and ethical standards.
- Negotiate supply contracts and service level agreements (SLAs).
- Monitor vendor performance and lead periodic reviews.
- Coordinate with Branch Leads to forecast procurement needs.
- Manage the corporate procurement pipeline and purchase order (PO) system.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Vendor Scorecards**** | Performance scorecards for corporate vendors. |
| ****Savings Reports**** | Quarterly reports on procurement cost savings. |
| ****Approved Vendor List**** | Master registry of verified and approved vendors. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Finance (FP&A)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Vendor Selection**** | Authority to approve vendors within budget. |
| ****Purchase Orders**** | Final sign-off on POs up to [THRESHOLD_X]. |
