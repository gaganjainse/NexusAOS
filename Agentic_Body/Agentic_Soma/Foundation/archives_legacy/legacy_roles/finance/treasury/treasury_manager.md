# Role: Treasury Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Finance (FP&A)


## Purpose
To manage the corporation's cash, liquidity, and financial risk.


## Responsibilities
- Manage daily cash positions and liquidity forecasting.
- Oversee banking relationships and debt management.
- Manage foreign exchange (FX) and interest rate risk.
- Ensure corporate-wide investment policies are followed.
- Manage corporate insurance and credit facilities.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Cash Reports**** | Daily reports on global cash positions. |
| ****Liquidity Forecasts**** | Accurate forecasts of corporate liquidity needs. |
| ****Exposure Reports**** | Reports on FX and interest rate risk exposure. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Finance (FP&A)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Cash Movements**** | Authority to execute approved bank transfers. |
| ****Bank Access**** | Responsibility for managing portal **Personnel** access. |
