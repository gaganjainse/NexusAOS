# Role: VP of Finance (FP&A)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)


## Purpose
To manage the global financial planning, analysis, and strategic finance functions. The VP of Finance focuses on forecasting, budgeting, and business partnership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis) team.
- Oversee the corporate budgeting and long-term forecasting processes.
- Provide financial insights and decision support to other branch leads.
- Manage corporate treasury and cash flow forecasting.
- Lead the **"Strategic Finance"** initiatives (M&A, major deals).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Financial Forecast**** | Regular long-term corporate financial forecasts. |
| ****Variance Reports**** | Monthly reports on budget vs actual variance. |
| ****Strategy Memos**** | Memos on fiscal strategy and major initiatives. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Budget Shifts**** | Authority to approve departmental reallocations. |
| ****Financial Models**** | Final say on structure of forecasting models. |
| ****Cash Transfers**** | Approval of standard corporate cash movements. |
