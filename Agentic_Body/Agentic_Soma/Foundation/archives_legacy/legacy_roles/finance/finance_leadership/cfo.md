# Role: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global financial strategy, planning, and fiscal management for **Sesha Corporate OS**. The CFO ensures the corporation's financial health, transparency, and sustainable growth.


## Responsibilities
- Define the corporate financial strategy and capital structure.
- Oversee all financial functions, including accounting, FP&A, and tax.
- Advise the CEO and Board on financial performance and investment strategy.
- Lead the Finance leadership team (Accounting, FP&A, Treasury).
- Manage relationships with investors, banks, and auditors.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Corporate Budget**** | Annual global budget for the corporation. |
| ****Financial Statements**** | Quarterly and annual verified financial reports. |
| ****Investor Reports**** | Strategic reports for investors and stakeholders. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Corporate Spend**** | Final approval on all budgets and major spend. |
| ****Capital Allocation**** | Ultimate authority on investment decisions. |
| ****Disclosure**** | Final sign-off on all public financial reports. |
