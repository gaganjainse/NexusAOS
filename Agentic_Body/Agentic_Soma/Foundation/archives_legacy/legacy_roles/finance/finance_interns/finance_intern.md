# Role: Finance Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** [Finance Manager


## Purpose
To learn professional finance and accounting practices while supporting the **Finance team**.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in account reconciliations and transaction recording.
- Support the FP&A team in data gathering and formatting.
- Help organize financial records and audit workpapers.
- Assist in the preparation of financial presentations.
- Conduct research on financial regulations or market trends.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Financial Folders**** | Organized folders for financial and audit records. |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Summaries of regulatory or market trend research. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a finance-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [Finance Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by a Finance Professional. |
