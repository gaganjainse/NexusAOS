# Role: Tax Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Corporate Controller


## Purpose
To lead the corporate tax strategy, compliance, and reporting functions.


## Responsibilities
- Manage corporate tax filings (Income, Sales, VAT/GST).
- Define the corporate tax strategy and transfer pricing policies.
- Manage external tax advisors and audits.
- Ensure compliance with local and international tax laws.
- Advise on tax implications of corporate deals and expansions.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Tax Filings**** | Finalized and filed corporate tax returns. |
| ****Provision Calcs**** | Detailed calculations for tax provisions. |
| ****Strategy Memos**** | Memos on tax strategy and impact assessments. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Corporate Controller
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Tax Filings**** | Final sign-off on standard tax returns. |
| ****Tax Positions**** | Authority over corporate tax interpretations. |
