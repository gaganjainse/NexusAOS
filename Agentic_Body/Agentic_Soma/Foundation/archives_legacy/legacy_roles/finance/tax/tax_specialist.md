# Role: Tax Specialist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Tax Manager


## Purpose
To perform the **Agentic calculations** and data gathering for tax compliance.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in the preparation of tax returns and schedules.
- Gather data for tax audits and information requests.
- Monitor tax payment deadlines and coordinate payments.
- Maintain corporate tax records and filings.
- Research routine tax issues and regulations.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Tax Schedules**** | Draft schedules and calculations for tax filings. |
| ****Evidence Packages**** | Organized evidence for tax audits and requests. |
| ****Compliance Logs**** | Updated logs tracking tax compliance status. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Tax Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Data Integrity**** | Responsibility for accuracy of tax calculations. |
| ****Filing Readiness**** | Initial authority to mark schedules as complete. |
