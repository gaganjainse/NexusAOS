# Role: Accounting Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Corporate Controller


## Purpose
To manage the daily operations of the accounting department and ensure accurate transaction recording.


## Responsibilities
- Oversee the accounts payable (AP) and accounts receivable (AR) teams.
- Manage the general ledger and reconcile complex accounts.
- Coordinate the month-end and year-end close activities.
- Review and approve standard journal entries and reconciliations.
- Assist in the preparation of financial statements.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****General Ledger**** | Finalized and closed corporate general ledger. |
| ****Reconciliation Reports**** | Verified reports for all corporate accounts. |
| ****Aging Reports**** | Accurate aging logs for payables and receivables. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Corporate Controller
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Journal Entries**** | Authority to approve routine adjustments. |
| ****Payment Batches**** | Initial sign-off on AP payment runs. |
