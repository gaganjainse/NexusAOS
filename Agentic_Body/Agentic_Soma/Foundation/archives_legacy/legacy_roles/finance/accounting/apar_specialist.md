# Role: AP/AR Specialist (Payable/Receivable)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Accountant


## Purpose
To handle the processing of vendor invoices (AP) and customer billing (AR).


## Responsibilities
- Process and record vendor invoices and **Personnel** expenses.
- Prepare customer invoices and follow up on overdue payments.
- Reconcile vendor statements and resolve payment issues.
- Maintain accurate AP and AR sub-ledgers.
- Respond to vendor and customer billing inquiries.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Invoice Batches**** | Processed and recorded batches of vendor invoices. |
| ****Billing Records**** | Accurate records of all customer billing activity. |
| ****Aging Logs**** | Daily logs tracking overdue payables and receivables. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Accountant
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Invoice Entry**** | Responsibility for accurate data entry. |
| ****Billing Integrity**** | Authority over correctness of assigned invoices. |
