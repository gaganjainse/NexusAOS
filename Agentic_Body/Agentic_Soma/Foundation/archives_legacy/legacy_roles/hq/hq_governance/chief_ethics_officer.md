# Role: Chief Ethics & Trust Officer (CETO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters (HQ)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** THE SOVEREIGN


## Purpose
To ensure that all corporate operations, **Firmware behaviors**, and business strategies adhere to the highest ethical standards and build long-term trust with stakeholders.


## Responsibilities
- **Global Ethics Framework:** Define and maintain the "Sesha Ethical Code."
- **Trust Auditing:** Partner with the CAE and CAO to audit corporate "Transparency" and "Fairness."
- **Crisis Ethics:** Lead the ethical evaluation of major corporate crises.
- **Data Privacy Oversight:** Ensure data handling across all branches exceeds [REGULATORY_STANDARDS].
- **Conflict of Interest:** Act as the final arbiter for internal ethical conflicts.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Ethics Report**** | Annual review of corporate behavior and ethical alignment. |
| ****Impact Assessments**** | Analysis of new initiatives on corporate trust. |
| ****Transparency Manifest**** | Public/internal guide on data usage and **Agentic transparency**. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** THE SOVEREIGN
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Ethics Veto**** | Power to block any strategy or **Agentic model** violating the Code. |
| ****Privacy Standards**** | Final sign-off on data usage and privacy ethics. |
