# Role: Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters / Corporate Control Layer
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Board of Directors


## Purpose
To execute the Board's vision by synchronizing the Systems and Audit branches. The CEO is responsible for the overall operational success and cultural health of the corporation.


## Responsibilities
- Translate Board-level strategy into actionable goals for Branch Heads.
- Mediate conflicts between Systems and Audit branches.
- Manage corporate-wide resource allocation.
- Ensure the corporation meets its sector compliance and ethical obligations.
- Lead the **"Corporate Control Layer"** meetings.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Operational Reports**** | Monthly summary of corporate health and KPIs. |
| ****Execution Roadmap**** | Quarterly roadmap for OS evolution. |
| ****Conflict Rulings**** | Ad-hoc rulings on cross-branch friction. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Board of Directors
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Cross-Branch Projects**** | Final sign-off on joint Systems/Audit initiatives. |
| ****Executive Hiring**** | Approval of all VP-level and above hires. |
| ****Strategic Pivots**** | Authority to adjust branch roadmaps. |
