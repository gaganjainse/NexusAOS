# Role: Chief of Staff
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters / Corporate Control Layer
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To act as a force multiplier for the CEO, managing strategic initiatives and cross-functional coordination at the HQ level.


## Responsibilities
- Lead special projects and strategic assignments for the CEO.
- Manage the CEO's office and executive communication flow.
- Facilitate the **"Corporate Control Layer"** meetings.
- Monitor the execution of CEO **Directives** across branches.
- Act as a buffer and advisor to the CEO on operational matters.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Executive Reports**** | Status updates on high-priority CEO initiatives. |
| ****Alignment Summaries**** | Briefings on branch-to-branch synergy. |
| ****Decision Memos**** | Briefing materials for executive sign-offs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Internal Handoffs**** | Authority to facilitate cross-branch project handoffs. |
| ****Agenda Control**** | Final say on the priority of items for HQ-level meetings. |
