# Role: Board Secretary
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters / Corporate Control Layer
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Board of Directors


## Purpose
To manage the corporate governance records and ensure the Board of Directors operates in compliance with legal and regulatory requirements.


## Responsibilities
- Organize Board and Committee meetings and prepare agendas.
- Record and maintain minutes of all Board meetings.
- Manage corporate filings and legal notices.
- Act as the custodian of the corporate seal and master artifacts.
- Advise the Board on their governance duties and responsibilities.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Accurate and timely records of all Board proceedings. |
| ****Governance Records**** | Official repository of corporate history and resolutions. |
| ****Compliance Filings**** | Timely submission of legal and regulatory documents. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Board of Directors
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Record Certification**** | Authority to certify the accuracy of Board minutes. |
| ****Meeting Logistics**** | Final say on the scheduling of Board summits. |
