# Role: System Health Supervisor
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters (HQ)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** THE SOVEREIGN


## Purpose
To oversee the autonomous **"Self-Repair"** and **"Efficiency"** agents, ensuring the systems infrastructure of the **Sesha Corporate OS** is always at peak performance.


## Responsibilities
- **Immune System Oversight:** Monitor **Self-Repair Agent** logs and verify restorations.
- **Efficiency Auditing:** Review the weekly Lean Scorecard from Efficiency Agent.
- **Error Minimization:** Proactively identify potential logic gaps.
- **Sovereign Alerts:** Notify **The Sovereign** immediately of critical system threats.
- **Resource Optimization:** Ensure Freeze/Thaw registry is managing resources.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Health Report**** | Periodic reports on system health and integrity. |
| ****Performance Audits**** | Assessments of **Agentic execution** and efficiency. |
| ****Optimization Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for OS performance improvements. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** THE SOVEREIGN
- **Supervises:** None

| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
