# Role: Sales Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Sales


## Purpose
To learn professional sales and revenue operations while supporting the Sales team.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in **Operational CRM** cleanup and lead enrichment.
- Help prepare sales presentations and proposals.
- Shadow discovery calls and customer success reviews.
- Conduct research on potential operational **Entity accounts**.
- Support the Sales Operations in reporting and documentation.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Enriched Lead Lists**** | Cleaned and enriched prospect data for sales. |
| ****Sales Summaries**** | Summaries of research on key accounts/segments. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a revenue-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Sales
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by a Sales Professional. |
