# Role: Head of Partnerships
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)


## Purpose
To build and manage strategic partnerships with operational hardware providers, labs, and other industry software companies.


## Responsibilities
- Define the partnership strategy and ecosystem roadmap.
- Identify and negotiate deals with key strategic partners.
- Manage the relationship with current partners and ensure mutual value.
- Coordinate with Systems on partnership integration **Agentic details**.
- Drive indirect revenue through partner referrals and co-selling.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Partnership Strategy**** | Strategic roadmap for ecosystem growth. |
| ****Partnership Agreements**** | Signed agreements with key industry partners. |
| ****Referral Reports**** | Reports on revenue generated from partner referrals. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Partner Selection**** | Authority over criteria for potential partners. |
| ****Integration Scope**** | Joint approval with CSO on **Agentic integrations**. |
| ****Joint Marketing**** | Approval of co-branded marketing initiatives. |
