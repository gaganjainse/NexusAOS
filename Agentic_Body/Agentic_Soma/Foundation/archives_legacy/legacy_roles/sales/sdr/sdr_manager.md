# Role: SDR Manager (Sales Development)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Sales


## Purpose
To lead the outbound prospecting and inbound lead qualification team. The SDR Manager ensures a steady flow of qualified opportunities for the Account Executives.


## Responsibilities
- Manage a team of Sales Development Representatives (SDRs).
- Define and optimize outbound prospecting cadences.
- Monitor SDR productivity (Calls, Emails, Meetings Booked).
- Ensure high-quality lead qualification according to BANT/similar criteria.
- Collaborate with Marketing on lead feedback and campaign alignment.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Performance Dashboards**** | Dashboards tracking SDR output and quality. |
| ****Prospecting Playbooks**** | Optimized scripts and cadences for outreach. |
| ****Lead Forecasts**** | Forecasts of qualified leads for the sales team. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Sales
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Lead Qualification**** | Final say on lead handoff readiness. |
| ****SDR Tooling**** | Approval of prospecting and outreach tools. |
