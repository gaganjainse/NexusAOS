# Role: Customer Success Manager (CSM)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Customer Success


## Purpose
To manage a portfolio of operational accounts and ensure they achieve their desired outcomes with **Sesha Corporate OS**.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the onboarding and training process for new operational customers.
- Monitor account health and proactively reach out to at-risk accounts.
- Conduct regular business reviews with key operational stakeholders.
- Identify and close upsell and expansion opportunities within accounts.
- Coordinate with Support on **Agentic** issues for their accounts.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Health Scorecards**** | Regular assessments of customer account health. |
| ****Success Plans**** | Documented plans for customer outcome achievement. |
| ****Expansion Pipeline**** | Pipeline of upsell and expansion opportunities. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Customer Success
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Onboarding Success**** | Authority to sign-off on onboarding completion. |
| ****Action Plans**** | Responsibility for customer-specific success plans. |
