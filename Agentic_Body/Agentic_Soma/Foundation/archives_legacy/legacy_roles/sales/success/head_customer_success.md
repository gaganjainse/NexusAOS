# Role: Head of Customer Success
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)


## Purpose
To ensure long-term customer satisfaction, high product adoption, and high renewal rates for **Sesha Corporate OS**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Customer Success team and strategy.
- Define customer onboarding and health monitoring processes.
- Manage customer churn and drive account expansions/upsells.
- Act as the **"Voice of the Customer"** within the corporation.
- Analyze customer feedback and usage data to inform the product roadmap.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Retention Dashboards**** | Live tracking of customer renewal and retention. |
| ****Churn Analysis**** | Deep-dive reports on the root causes of account churn. |
| ****CS Playbooks**** | Standardized protocols for customer success interactions. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Account Risk Status**** | Final say on account "Risk" or "Health" status. |
| ****CS Playbooks**** | Authority over customer interaction protocols. |
| ****Renewal Terms**** | Sign-off on standard account renewal contracts. |
