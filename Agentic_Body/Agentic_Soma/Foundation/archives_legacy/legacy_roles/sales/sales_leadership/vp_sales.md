# Role: VP of Sales
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)


## Purpose
To manage the global sales team and ensure the achievement of revenue targets. The VP of Sales focuses on the "How we sell" and the efficiency of the sales engine.


## Responsibilities
- Manage regional sales managers and account executives.
- Oversee the sales pipeline and forecasting.
- Drive sales productivity and methodology.
- Collaborate with Marketing on lead generation and quality.
- Manage sales recruitment and team performance.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Pipeline Reports**** | Weekly reporting on global pipeline and deal flow. |
| ****Scorecards**** | Regional performance and productivity scorecards. |
| ****Enablement Strategy**** | Global strategy for sales tools and training. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Discounting**** | Authority to approve standard pricing discounts. |
| ****Sales Hiring**** | Approval of AE and Sales Manager hires. |
| ****Pipeline Stages**** | Final say on stage transition criteria. |
