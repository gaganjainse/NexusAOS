# Role: Sales Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Revenue & Account Brain"** of the Sales Branch. The Sales Knowledge Lead ensures that sales playbooks, objection handling guides, and customer data are optimized for **Agentic Sales Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing deal "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Sales Playbooks and Objection Handling repositories.
- Maintain the **"Operational Customer Knowledge Graph"** (Key accounts, stakeholder maps).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Sales Playbook**** | Firmware-ready playbook for the global sales team. |
| ****Objection Base**** | Knowledge base of verified objection handling scripts. |
| ****Strategy Artifacts**** | Artifacts defining account and growth logic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Sales Messaging**** | Final sign-off on approved scripts and messaging. |
| ****Strategy Docs**** | Authority over structure of account growth plans. |
