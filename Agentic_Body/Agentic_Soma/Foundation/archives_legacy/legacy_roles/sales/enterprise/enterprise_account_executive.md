# Role: Enterprise Account Executive (EAE)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Enterprise Sales Manager


## Purpose
To identify, negotiate, and close high-value sales deals with large industry **Entities**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the full sales cycle for large-scale enterprise accounts.
- Conduct deep discovery and product demonstrations for complex stakeholders.
- Navigate multi-stakeholder approval processes (Operational, **Agentic**, Procurement).
- Coordinate with Sales Engineering on **Agentic setup** requirements.
- Meet or exceed enterprise sales quotas.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Enterprise Contracts**** | Executed high-value agreements with large **Entities**. |
| ****Entity Maps**** | Detailed maps of stakeholders within key accounts. |
| ****Accuracy Reports**** | Forecast accuracy reports for enterprise pipeline. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Enterprise Sales Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Deal Strategy**** | Authority over tactical approach to assigned deals. |
| ****Stakeholder Access**** | Responsibility for navigating key account stakeholders. |
