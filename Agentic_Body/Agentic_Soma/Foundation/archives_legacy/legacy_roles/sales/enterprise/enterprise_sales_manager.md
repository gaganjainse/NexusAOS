# Role: Enterprise Sales Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Sales


## Purpose
To lead the sales efforts targeting large industry hubs, core business units, and sector **Entities**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage a team of Enterprise Account Executives.
- Oversee complex, long-cycle sales processes.
- Lead the strategy for key account acquisition and growth.
- Provide coaching and support for high-stakes enterprise negotiations.
- Collaborate with Legal and Finance on enterprise contract terms.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Pipeline Report**** | Regular reporting on enterprise sales pipeline health. |
| ****Strategy Docs**** | Multi-year strategic plans for key account acquisition. |
| ****Win/Loss Analysis**** | Detailed analysis of enterprise deal outcomes. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Sales
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Proposals**** | Initial approval on enterprise-level sales proposals. |
| ****Resource Allocation**** | Authority to assign **Agentic support** to key deals. |
| ****Account Assignment**** | Decision on distribution of enterprise leads. |
