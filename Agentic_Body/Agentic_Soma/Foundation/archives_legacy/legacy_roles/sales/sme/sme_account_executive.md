# Role: SME Account Executive (SAE)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** SME Sales Manager


## Purpose
To manage and close sales deals with small and medium-sized core business units.


## Responsibilities
- Manage a high volume of sales opportunities in the SME segment.
- Conduct standard product demonstrations and trials.
- Close deals quickly using established sales playbooks.
- Handle common operational objections and pricing negotiations.
- Maintain accurate **Operational CRM** records for all SME opportunities.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Signed SME Contracts**** | Executed contracts for small/medium business deals. |
| ****Sales Volume Reports**** | Monthly reports on volume of closed deals. |
| ****Database Logs**** | Real-time activity logs in the business database. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** SME Sales Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Sales Presentation**** | Authority over delivery of standard presentations. |
| ****Price Adjustments**** | Responsibility for applying approved discounts. |
