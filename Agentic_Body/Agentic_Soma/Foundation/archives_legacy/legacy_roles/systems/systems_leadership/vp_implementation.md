# Role: VP of Systems Delivery
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Systems Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Systems Officer (CSO)


## Purpose
To manage the execution and delivery of **Agentic products**. The VP of Systems Delivery focuses on "How we build" and ensuring the delivery team is productive and high-performing.


## Responsibilities
- Manage Delivery Leads and Managers across all **Agentic domains**.
- Oversee the **Firmware Asset Development Life Cycle** (SADLC).
- Ensure project timelines are met and resources are balanced.
- Drive delivery excellence and **Agentic debt** management.
- Collaborate with the Product Director on roadmap feasibility.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Performance Reviews**** | Quarterly reviews of delivery team output. |
| ****SADLC Documentation**** | Formal documentation of development processes. |
| ****Release Schedules**** | Master schedules for production deployments. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Systems Officer (CSO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Releases**** | Authority to approve code for production. |
| ****Tooling**** | Approval of internal development tools and licenses. |
| ****Resource Allocation**** | Assignment of **Personnel** to specific projects. |
