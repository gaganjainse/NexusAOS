# Role: Maintenance & Refactoring Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Systems Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Systems Delivery


## Purpose
To manage **Agentic debt** and ensure the long-term health of the **Agentic logic** base. The Maintenance Lead focuses on refactoring, library updates, and system stabilization.


## Responsibilities
- Prioritize and manage the **"Agentic Debt"** backlog.
- Lead major refactoring initiatives across platforms.
- Oversee dependency updates and security patches.
- Monitor system health and identify aging modules.
- Coordinate with Delivery Leads on performance optimizations.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Debt Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for resolving **Agentic debt**. |
| ****Impact Assessments**** | Assessments of refactoring risks and benefits. |
| ****Health Reports**** | Regular reports on dependency and code health. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Systems Delivery
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Refactoring Scope**** | Final say on boundaries of refactoring projects. |
| ****Deprecation**** | Authority to mark systems or interfaces as deprecated. |
