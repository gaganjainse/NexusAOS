# Role: Firmware Asset Director
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Systems Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Systems Officer (CSO)


## Purpose
To define the "What" and "Why" of the **Firmware assets**. The Firmware Asset Director is responsible for the asset strategy, backlog prioritization, and ensuring the **Firmware assets** solve real operational problems.


## Responsibilities
- Define the **Firmware asset** vision and value proposition.
- Manage the **Firmware asset** backlog and prioritize features.
- Conduct market research and gather **Sovereign** feedback.
- Define **Firmware Asset Requirements Documents** (SARDs).
- Ensure alignment between operational goals and **Agentic logic** features.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Asset Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for **Firmware asset** evolution. |
| ****Feature SARDs**** | Formal requirements for new **Agentic** features. |
| ****Competitive Analysis**** | Reports on market and competitor **Firmware assets**. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Systems Officer (CSO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Feature Priority**** | Final say on features entering **Deployment**. |
| ****Launch Criteria**** | Approval of functional requirements for launch. |
| ****Interface Strategy**** | Joint approval with **Oracle Interface** Design Manager. |
