# Role: Legal Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Senior Legal Counsel


## Purpose
To learn professional legal and corporate practices while supporting the Legal team.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in legal research and drafting of memos.
- Support contract management and filing processes.
- Help organize and review corporate records.
- Observe negotiations and legal strategy sessions.
- Complete a legal-focused internship project.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Memos**** | Preliminary research on assigned legal topics. |
| ****Record Files**** | Organized folders for corporate and legal records. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a technical legal topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Senior Legal Counsel
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by an Attorney. |
