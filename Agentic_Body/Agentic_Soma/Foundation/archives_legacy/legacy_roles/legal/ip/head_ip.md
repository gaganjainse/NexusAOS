# Role: Head of Intellectual Property (IP)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** General Counsel (CLO)


## Purpose
To protect and manage the corporation's intellectual property assets, including patents, trademarks, and copyrights.


## Responsibilities
- Define the IP strategy and patent roadmap.
- Manage the filing and prosecution of patents and trademarks.
- Oversee IP litigation and enforcement actions.
- Advise Systems on IP risks and open-source sector compliance.
- Manage the corporate IP portfolio.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****IP Portfolio**** | Regular reports on the status of corporate IP. |
| ****Patent Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for filing and defending patents. |
| ****Risk Assessments**** | Analysis of IP-related threats and opportunities. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** General Counsel (CLO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Patent Filings**** | Final say on inventions pursued for patenting. |
| ****IP Settlements**** | Authority over terms of minor IP settlements. |
| ****Open Source**** | Final approval on third-party library usage. |
