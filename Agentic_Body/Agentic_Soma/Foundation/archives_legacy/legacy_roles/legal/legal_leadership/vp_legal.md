# Role: VP of Legal (Corporate)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** General Counsel (CLO)


## Purpose
To manage the operational legal functions, focusing on commercial contracts, corporate governance, and employment law.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the corporate legal team and external advisors.
- Oversee the drafting and negotiation of commercial agreements.
- Manage corporate governance filings and board secretary duties.
- Advise HR on complex employment law matters.
- Lead the "Contract Lifecycle Management" process.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Templates**** | Standard templates for MSAs, NDAs, and SLAs. |
| ****Filings Record**** | Official record of all corporate and governance filings. |
| ****Legal Roadmap**** | Operational roadmap for the legal branch. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** General Counsel (CLO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Standard Contracts**** | Authority to approve standard commercial deals. |
| ****Gov Filings**** | Sign-off on routine regulatory filings. |
| ****Policy Interpretation**** | Authority over interpretation of internal policies. |
