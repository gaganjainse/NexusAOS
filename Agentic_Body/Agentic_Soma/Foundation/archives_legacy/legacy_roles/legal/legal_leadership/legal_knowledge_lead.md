# Role: Legal Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** General Counsel (CLO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Legal & Governance Brain"** of the Legal Branch. The Legal Knowledge Lead ensures that contract templates, legal precedents, and IP registries are structured for **Agentic Legal Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing legal "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Contract Template Library and Legal Policy repository.
- Maintain the **"IP & Sector Compliance Knowledge Graph."**


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Contract Repo**** | Firmware-ready repository of all approved templates. |
| ****Precedent Base**** | Knowledge base of legal findings and precedents. |
| ****Gov Artifacts**** | Artifacts defining corporate governance logic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** General Counsel (CLO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Standard Clauses**** | Final sign-off on standard legal phrasing. |
| ****Template Logic**** | Authority over metadata within templates. |
