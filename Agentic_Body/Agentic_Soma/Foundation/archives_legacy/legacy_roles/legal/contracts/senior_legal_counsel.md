# Role: Senior Legal Counsel
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Legal (Corporate)


## Purpose
To handle complex legal matters and provide expert advice on commercial, corporate, and regulatory issues.


## Responsibilities
- Negotiate high-value and complex sales and partnership contracts.
- Advise senior management on legal risks and opportunities.
- Manage complex litigation and dispute resolution processes.
- Mentor junior legal **Personnel** and paralegals.
- Conduct internal legal training for other branches.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Key Contracts**** | Negotiated high-value commercial agreements. |
| ****Advisory Memos**** | Expert advice on complex corporate and legal risks. |
| ****Litigation Status**** | Regular reports on the status of legal disputes. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Legal (Corporate)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Contract Clauses**** | Authority over custom and complex legal wording. |
| ****Precedents**** | Final say on internal interpretation of case law. |
