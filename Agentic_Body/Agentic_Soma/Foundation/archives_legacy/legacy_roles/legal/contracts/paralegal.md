# Role: Paralegal
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Senior Legal Counsel


## Purpose
To provide substantive legal support through research, document preparation, and case management.


## Responsibilities
- Conduct legal research on specific statutes, cases, and regulations.
- Prepare first drafts of legal documents and pleadings.
- Organize and manage litigation files and discovery documents.
- Support corporate and board secretary functions.
- Assist in the preparation of board and committee materials.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Summaries of statute and case-law research. |
| ****Case Files**** | Organized litigation and discovery file sets. |
| ****Corporate Minutes**** | Drafted minutes for board and committee syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Senior Legal Counsel
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Accuracy**** | Responsibility for factual integrity of research. |
| ****Organization**** | Authority over structure of assigned legal files. |
