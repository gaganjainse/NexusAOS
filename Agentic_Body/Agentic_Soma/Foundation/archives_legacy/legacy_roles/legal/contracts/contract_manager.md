# Role: Contract Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Legal (Corporate)


## Purpose
To manage the end-to-end lifecycle of corporate contracts and ensure efficient drafting and execution.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Contract Management System (CMS).
- Coordinate the contract review process across departments.
- Track contract renewals, expirations, and obligations.
- Assist in the drafting of standard agreements (NDAs, SOWs).
- Maintain the repository of executed contracts.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Status Dashboard**** | Live dashboard of all active and pending contracts. |
| ****Standard Agreements**** | Drafted and approved contract templates. |
| ****Obligation Reports**** | Reports tracking corporate contractual duties. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Legal (Corporate)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****CMS Workflow**** | Authority over internal contract approval routing. |
| ****Document Integrity**** | Responsibility for accuracy of contract metadata. |
