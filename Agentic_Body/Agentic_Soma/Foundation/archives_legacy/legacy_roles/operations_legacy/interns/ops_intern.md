# Role: Operations Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Internal Systems Manager (Internal Services)


## Purpose
To learn corporate operations and internal **Agentic** practices while supporting the Operations team.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in internal **Agentic support** and hardware prep.
- Support facility maintenance tracking and organization.
- Help with operational data entry and reporting.
- Conduct research on operational efficiency or green office practices.
- Complete an operations-focused internship project.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Inventory Logs**** | Updated logs of corporate assets and supplies. |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Summaries of operational efficiency research. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on an operations-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Internal Systems Manager (Internal Services)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by an Ops Professional. |
