# Role: Internal Systems Specialist (Internal Support)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Internal Systems Manager (Internal Services)


## Purpose
To provide hands-on **Agentic support** and administration for the internal organization.


## Responsibilities
- Resolve internal **Agentic support** tickets and functional issues.
- Administer internal SaaS tools and directory services (e.g., Okta, Google Workspace).
- Setup and configure new hire functional assets.
- Monitor internal **Agentic infrastructure** for performance and security.
- Assist in **Agentic [CORE_INFRASTRUCTURE]** projects and deployments.
