# Role: Chief Operating Officer (COO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global operational strategy and ensure the efficient daily functioning of **Sesha Corporate OS**. The COO focuses on **"Operational Excellence"** and scaling the company's internal capabilities.


## Responsibilities
- Define the corporate operational strategy and budget.
- Oversee all operational functions, including Internal **Agentic Systems**, Facilities, and Supply Chain.
- Drive corporate-wide process optimization and efficiency.
- Lead the Operations leadership team.
- Ensure the physical and **Agentic infrastructure** supports the company's growth.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Operational Plan**** | Annual strategic plan for global operations. |
| ****Efficiency Reports**** | Quarterly reports on corporate process optimization. |
| ****Resource Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for resource management and scaling. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Ops Spend**** | Final approval on operational/**Agentic** budgets. |
| ****Process Standards**** | Ultimate authority on corporate-wide operational SOPs. |
| ****Vendor Selection**** | Approval of all major operational service providers. |
