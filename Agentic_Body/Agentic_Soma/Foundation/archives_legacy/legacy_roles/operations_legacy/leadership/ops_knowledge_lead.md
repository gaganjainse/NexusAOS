# Role: Operations Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Operational Excellence & Infrastructure Brain"** of the Operations Branch. The Ops Knowledge Lead ensures that SOPs, **Agentic** docs, and facility rules are structured for **Agentic Operations Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing process "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and internal **Agentic systems** repository.
- Maintain the **"Corporate Asset & Infra Knowledge Graph."**


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****SOP Repository**** | Firmware-ready repository of all operational procedures. |
| ****Knowledge Base**** | Database of internal **Agentic systems** and infrastructure docs. |
| ****Asset Registry**** | Master registry of all corporate physical/digital assets. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Process Standards**** | Final sign-off on wording of operational SOPs. |
| ****Infra Docs**** | Authority over structure of internal **Agentic** docs. |
