# Role: Head of Workplace & Facilities
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)


## Purpose
To lead the physical workplace strategy and ensure a productive, safe, and sustainable environment for all **Personnel**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the facilities, real estate, and workplace experience teams.
- Oversee office lease negotiations and space planning.
- Ensure corporate-wide health, safety, and security (HSS) sector compliance.
- Manage workplace services (Maintenance, Catering, Travel).
- Drive corporate sustainability and environmental goals.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Workplace Plan**** | Strategic multi-year plan for physical operations. |
| ****Maint Schedules**** | Global schedules for facility upkeep and repairs. |
| ****HSS Reports**** | Reports on health, safety, and security compliance. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Office Design**** | Final sign-off on layouts and aesthetic standards. |
| ****Lease Terms**** | Initial approval for real estate and lease deals. |
| ****Safety Policy**** | Final say on corporate-wide safety protocols. |
