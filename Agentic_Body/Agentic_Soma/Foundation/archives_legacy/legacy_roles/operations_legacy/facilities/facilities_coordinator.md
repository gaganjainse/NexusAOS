# Role: Facilities Coordinator
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Facilities Manager


## Purpose
To handle the logistical and administrative details of office and facility operations.


## Responsibilities
- Coordinate with maintenance and cleaning vendors.
- Manage office supply inventory and procurement.
- Support office move and renovation logistics.
- Monitor and report on facility issues (lighting, plumbing, etc.).
- Assist in the organization of workplace safety training.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Facility Logs**** | Daily logs of facility issues and maintenance. |
| ****Inventory Reports**** | Monthly reports on office supply status. |
| ****Vendor Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for maintenance providers. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Facilities Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Supply Ordering**** | Authority to order supplies within budget limits. |
| ****Vendor Access**** | Responsibility for coordinating provider entry. |
