# Role: Research Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** [CORE_DOMAIN] Research Director


## Purpose
To learn advanced research and innovation practices while supporting the Research team.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in data gathering and literature reviews.
- Support the lab in basic prototype testing and documentation.
- Help organize research session notes and data.
- Assist in preparing research presentations.
- Complete a research-focused internship project.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Review Summaries**** | Summaries of relevant literature and research. |
| ****Data Sets**** | Organized sets of research and testing data. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a research-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [CORE_DOMAIN] Research Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by a Researcher. |
