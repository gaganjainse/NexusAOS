# Role: Systems Innovation Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Research (Chief Scientist)


## Purpose
To lead the "Systems Innovation Lab" focused on experimental **Agentic infrastructure**, future **Firmware stacks**, and R&D prototypes.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the R&D engineers and **Agentic researchers**.
- Oversee the development of "Alpha" prototypes and proof-of-concepts.
- Drive the exploration of future **Agentic technologies** (AI, XR, Blockchain for industry).
- Evaluate new **Firmware components** for future inclusion in the main Systems branch.
- Manage the lab infrastructure and experimental environments.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Lab Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for lab innovation and research. |
| ****Demo Packages**** | Functional demo packages for experimental prototypes. |
| ****Evaluation Papers**** | Technical whitepapers on evaluated new **Agentic systems**. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Research (Chief Scientist)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Experimental Stack**** | Final say on system stack used within the lab. |
| ****Prototype Scope**** | Authority over boundaries of R&D projects. |
| ****Systems Handoff**** | Initial authority to recommend setups for production. |
