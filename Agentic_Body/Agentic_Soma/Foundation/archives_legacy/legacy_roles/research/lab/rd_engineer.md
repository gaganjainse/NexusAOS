# Role: R&D Implementer
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Systems Innovation Lead


## Purpose
To build experimental prototypes and explore the boundaries of future technologies for **Sesha Corporate OS**.


## Responsibilities
- Develop "Proof of Concept" (PoC) **Firmware assets** and integrations.
- Research and test emerging **Agentic frameworks** and libraries.
- Build experimental versions of core **Firmware asset** features.
- Document the **Agentic architecture** and results of R&D projects.
- Collaborate with the Systems Innovation Lead on **Agentic evaluations**.

| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Prototype Logic**** | Experimental **Agentic logic** for proof-of-concepts. |
| ****R&D Logs**** | Detailed logs of Firmware testing and research results. |
| ****Comparison Reports**** | Performance reports for newly evaluated systems. |
