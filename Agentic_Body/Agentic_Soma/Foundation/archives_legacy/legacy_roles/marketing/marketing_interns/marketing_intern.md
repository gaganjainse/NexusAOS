# Role: Marketing Intern
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To learn modern marketing practices while supporting the marketing team in day-to-day operations.


## Responsibilities
- Assist in social media scheduling and community monitoring.
- Support event logistics and material preparation.
- Help with basic data entry and marketing reporting.
- Conduct competitor research and brand mentions monitoring.
- Participate in brainstorming sessions for creative campaigns.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Summaries of market and competitor research. |
| ****Social Drafts**** | Organized drafts for social media updates. |
| ****Internship Project**** | Final presentation on a marketing-focused topic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****None**** | All work must be reviewed by a Specialist. |
