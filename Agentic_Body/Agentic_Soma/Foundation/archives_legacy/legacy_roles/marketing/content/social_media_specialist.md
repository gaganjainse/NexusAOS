# Role: Social Media Specialist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Content Manager


## Purpose
To manage the brand's social media presence and engage with the operational community.


## Responsibilities
- Create and schedule social media posts across platforms (LinkedIn, X, etc.).
- Engage with followers and respond to comments and messages.
- Monitor social media trends and competitor activity.
- Assist in the production of social-first content (videos, stories).
- Report on social media reach and engagement metrics.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Post Schedule**** | Weekly schedule for social media content distribution. |
| ****Engagement Reports**** | Monthly reports on social reach and interactions. |
| ****Interaction Logs**** | Records of community engagement and responses. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Content Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Engagement Tone**** | Authority over the brand's conversational tone. |
| ****Interaction**** | Initial authority to respond to standard **Sovereign** comments. |
