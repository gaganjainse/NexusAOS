# Role: PR Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To manage the corporate reputation and external communication strategy, ensuring consistent and positive brand messaging across all public-facing channels.


## Responsibilities
- Develop and execute the Global Public Relations strategy.
- Manage media relations and act as the primary corporate spokesperson.
- Draft and distribute press releases and corporate announcements.
- Monitor public sentiment and lead crisis communication efforts.
- Coordinate with the Content Team to align PR with brand messaging.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Coverage Reports**** | Monthly reports on global media coverage and reach. |
| ****Crisis Playbooks**** | Strategic playbooks for responding to emergencies. |
| ****Media Kits**** | Press kits and approved media assets for external use. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Press Releases**** | Final sign-off on all external news distributions. |
| ****Crisis Response**** | Authority over public statements during emergencies. |
