# Role: Content Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To lead the content marketing strategy and production for **Sesha Corporate OS**. The Content Manager ensures the brand provides value through high-quality information.


## Responsibilities
- Define the content calendar and distribution strategy.
- Oversee the production of blogs, whitepapers, and case studies.
- Manage the social media presence and community engagement.
- Coordinate with Product for **Agentic** content accuracy.
- Manage external content contributors and writers.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Content Calendar**** | Strategic schedule for all marketing content. |
| ****Articles**** | Published whitepapers, blogs, and case studies. |
| ****Engagement Reports**** | Reports on social media and content reach. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Editorial Calendar**** | Final say on the corporate publication schedule. |
| ****External Writers**** | Authority to onboard and manage freelance talent. |
| ****Social Posts**** | Sign-off on brand social media messaging. |
