# Role: Content Specialist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Content Manager


## Purpose
To create and distribute valuable content to attract and retain the operational audience.


## Responsibilities
- Write blog posts, newsletters, and email sequences.
- Produce basic visual assets and infographics.
- Manage content distribution across internal and external channels.
- Interview subject matter experts for content creation.
- Support the Content Manager in content reporting.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Content Pieces**** | Draft and final articles, newsletters, and emails. |
| ****Performance Data**** | Reports on content reach and engagement metrics. |
| ****Schedules**** | Distribution schedules for all marketing channels. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Content Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Content Drafts**** | Responsibility for factual and grammatical quality. |
| ****Email Formatting**** | Authority over visual layout of assigned newsletters. |
