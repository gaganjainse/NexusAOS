# Role: Marketing Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Brand & Growth Brain"** of the Marketing Branch. The Marketing Knowledge Lead ensures that brand guidelines, campaign data, and market insights are structured for **Agentic Firmware** and human teams.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing campaign "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Brand Identity System and Marketing Guidelines.
- Manage the **"Marketing Knowledge Graph"** (**Entity** segments, competitor profiles).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Brand Guidelines**** | Firmware-ready identity and voice guidelines. |
| ****Insights Repository**** | Repository for verified market research insights. |
| ****Performance Base**** | Knowledge base of campaign results and success data. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Brand Standards**** | Final sign-off on updates to voice and tone. |
| ****Market Data**** | Authority over the "Truth" status of research. |
