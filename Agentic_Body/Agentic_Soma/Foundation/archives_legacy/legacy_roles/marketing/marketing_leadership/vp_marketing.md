# Role: VP of Marketing
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)


## Purpose
To execute the marketing strategy and manage the operational marketing departments. The VP of Marketing focuses on "How we grow" and ensuring marketing KPIs are met.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the performance marketing, content, and events departments.
- Oversee the execution of marketing campaigns.
- Analyze marketing ROI and adjust tactics accordingly.
- Collaborate with Sales on lead generation and enablement.
- Manage the internal marketing operations and team culture.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Performance Reports**** | Monthly summaries of global marketing ROI and KPIs. |
| ****Campaign Post-Mortems**** | Detailed analysis of completed campaign results. |
| ****Marketing Roadmap**** | Operational roadmap for marketing initiatives. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Campaign Launch**** | Approval for individual marketing executions. |
| ****Agentic Stack**** | Selection of automation and analytics tools. |
| ****Lead Flow**** | Authority over lead qualification and handoff logic. |
