# Role: Graphic Designer
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Art Director


## Purpose
To create visual designs for marketing collateral across all channels.


## Responsibilities
- Design ads, social media graphics, and website banners.
- Create brochures, flyers, and other print materials.
- Assist in maintaining the design system's marketing components.
- Prepare design files for professional printing or digital use.
- Collaborate with copywriters on design/copy integration.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Graphic Designs**** | High-fidelity designs for all marketing channels. |
| ****Print-ready Files**** | Formatted and verified files for production. |
| ****Asset Libraries**** | Maintained libraries of design components. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Art Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Visual Execution**** | Responsibility for aesthetic quality of assignments. |
| ****File Integrity**** | Authority over **Agentic** structure of design files. |
