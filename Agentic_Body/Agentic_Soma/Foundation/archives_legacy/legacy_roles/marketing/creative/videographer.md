# Role: Videographer / Motion Designer
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Art Director


## Purpose
To produce high-quality video content and motion graphics for marketing campaigns.


## Responsibilities
- Shoot and edit promotional videos and product demos.
- Create motion graphics and animations for digital ads.
- Manage video production equipment and studio space.
- Optimize video content for various social and web platforms.
- Assist in the storytelling and storyboarding process.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Video Files**** | Final edited video files for distribution. |
| ****Motion Assets**** | High-quality graphics and animations for ads. |
| ****Production Archives**** | B-roll and source files from video shoots. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Art Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Edit Selection**** | Responsibility for creative quality of video edits. |
| ****Agentic Quality**** | Authority over resolution and compression specs. |
