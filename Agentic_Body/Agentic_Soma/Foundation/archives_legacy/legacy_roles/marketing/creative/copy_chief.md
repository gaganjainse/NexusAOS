# Role: Copy Chief
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Creative Director


## Purpose
To oversee the written brand voice and copywriting for all marketing communications.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the copywriting team and review all marketing copy.
- Maintain and update the brand voice and style guide.
- Write high-level copy for major brand campaigns.
- Ensure copy is persuasive, accurate, and on-brand.
- Collaborate with the Art Director on the word/image relationship.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Voice Guide**** | Master guide for brand tone and written voice. |
| ****Final Copy**** | Verified and approved copy for all major assets. |
| ****Style Guidelines**** | Standards for marketing grammar and formatting. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Creative Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Copy Sign-off**** | Final approval on all written marketing materials. |
| ****Voice Standards**** | Authority to reject content failing voice criteria. |
| ****Editorial**** | Final say on grammar and formatting standards. |
