# Role: Creative Director
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)


## Purpose
To lead the creative vision for all marketing and brand communications. The Creative Director ensures that all visual and written content is high-quality and on-brand.


## Responsibilities
- Direct the creative process for all marketing assets (Video, Web, Print).
- Lead the creative team and external freelancers.
- Ensure creative consistency across all marketing touchpoints.
- Manage the production of high-level creative campaigns.
- Present creative concepts to the CMO and CEO.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Identity Systems**** | Master visual and verbal identity frameworks. |
| ****Campaign Concepts**** | High-level creative concepts for global initiatives. |
| ****Production Schedules**** | Master schedules for creative asset delivery. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Visual Assets**** | Final sign-off on creative assets before publication. |
| ****Creative Talent**** | Selection and management of external creatives. |
| ****Brand Aesthetics**** | Ultimate authority over brand guideline execution. |
