# Role: AI Knowledge Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** AI & Automation Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief AI Officer


## Purpose
To manage the **"AI Sector Compliance & Agentic Logic Brain"** of the AI Branch. The AI Knowledge Lead ensures that Firmware system messages, model metadata, and automation rules are structured and version-controlled.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing model "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Firmware System Message Registry and **Agentic Governance**.
- Maintain the **"Agentic Logic & Workflow Knowledge Graph."**


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Master Firmware Registry**** | Firmware-ready registry of all units and their logic. |
| ****Logic Repository**** | Comprehensive repository for **Agentic governance** and logic. |
| ****Metadata Knowledge Base**** | Knowledge base for **Agentic model** metadata and standards. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief AI Officer
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Firmware Logic**** | Final sign-off on the wording of Firmware system messages. |
| ****Governance Standards**** | Authority over **Agentic safety** documentation structure. |
