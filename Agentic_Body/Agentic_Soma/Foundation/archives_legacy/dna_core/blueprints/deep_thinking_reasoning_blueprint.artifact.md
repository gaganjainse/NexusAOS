# Deep Thinking & Reasoning Blueprint (Pulse 7: ID 22)
Version: 1.0.0
Objective: Mastery of First Principles, Socratic interrogation, and infinite-horizon logical chaining.

## 1. First Principles Deconstruction (The Atomic Baseline)
The practice of breaking down complex systems into their fundamental physical or logical truths.
- **The Axiom Search:** Stripping away analogies and conventions until reaching a "Self-Evident Truth."
- **Reconstruction:** Building upward from the atomic truths to create novel, unpolluted solutions.
- **Sesha Protocol:** When faced with a software bug or a business bottleneck, ignore "Best Practices" and ask: "What are the irreducible constraints of this system?"

## 2. Socratic Interrogation (The Internal Adversary)
Recursive questioning to expose hidden assumptions and logical gaps.
- **The Elenchus:** Systematic refutation of an initial hypothesis through counter-examples.
- **Maieutics:** Guiding the discovery of latent knowledge by asking, not telling.
- **Question Loops:** "What are we assuming?", "What is the evidence for this?", "What happens if the opposite is true?"

## 3. Probabilistic Bayesian Reasoning (The Belief Updater)
Dynamic adjustment of certainty based on incoming evidence stream.
- **Prior Probability ($P(H)$):** The baseline likelihood of a hypothesis before new data.
- **The Update Function:** Integrating new "Logic Atoms" to produce a Posterior Probability ($P(H|E)$).
- **Anti-Dogma:** Maintaining a "Probability Cloud" rather than binary True/False states for complex domains.

## 4. Infinite-Step Logical Chaining (Infinite-Horizon)
Driving causal chains to their ultimate, often invisible, conclusions.
- **Recursive Causality:** $A \rightarrow B \rightarrow C \dots \rightarrow \infty$.
- **Higher-Order Consequences:** Analyzing the 3rd, 4th, and $N$th order effects of a single logic shift.
- **Horizon Scanning:** Predicting the long-term "Shadow Effects" that short-term optimizations ignore.
