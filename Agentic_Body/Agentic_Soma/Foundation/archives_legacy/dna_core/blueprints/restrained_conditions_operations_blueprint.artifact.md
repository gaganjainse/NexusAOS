# Working under Restrained Conditions Blueprint (Singularity Ingestion)
Version: 1.0.0
Description: The DNA for survivalist computing and "Degraded Mode" operations under extreme resource scarcity.

## 1. "Degraded Mode" Protocols
- **Critical-Vitals Only:** Shutting down 95% of the soma to keep the "Brain" and "Kernel" alive on <1% battery.
- **512MB RAM Survival:** Dynamic memory swapping and aggressive logic-atom pruning to prevent OOM (Out of Memory) crashes.

## 2. Zero-Wake Execution
- **Passive Monitoring:** Using hardware-based timers and low-power E-cores for sensing, only waking the full Agentic Body when a "Sovereign Salience" event is detected.
- **Direct-to-Disk Buffering:** Writing results to persistent storage immediately to prevent data loss during power failure.

## 3. Direct Boot & Recovery
- **Immutable Core:** Maintaining a minimalist "Safe-Mode" version of Sesha in the EFI partition for instant recovery from system-level failure.
- **Survivalist Computing:** Operating without internet, cloud, or external power—achieving absolute local sovereignty.
