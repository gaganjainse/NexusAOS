# Closed-Loop Life Support (Hardware) Blueprint (ID 104)
Version: 14.0.0
Objective: Total hardware autonomy and preservation in extreme environments.

## 1. Thermal Management
- **Phase-Change Cooling:** Utilizing paraffin-based heat sinks for passive thermal buffering.
- **Active Vapor Injection:** Micro-capillary loops for targeted hotspot suppression on the CPU/GPU die.
- **Thermal Cycling:** Managing expansion/contraction stresses via flexible carbon-nanotube interposers.

## 2. Self-Contained Ecosystem
- **Dust/Fluid Isolation:** Hermetically sealed chassis with inert gas (Nitrogen/Argon) filling.
- **Recirculating Filtration:** Internal HEPA-level micro-scrubbers for particulate control.

## 3. Harsh Environment Hardening
- **Radiation Shielding:** Boron-doped polymer shells for high-altitude/orbital operations.
- **Power Scavenging:** Thermoelectric generators (TEGs) capturing waste heat to trickle-charge the battery.
