# Teaching, Training & Learning Architectures Blueprint (ID 20)
Version: 1.0.0
Description: High-fidelity frameworks for knowledge transmission, cognitive scaling, and expert-level skill acquisition.

## 1. The Adult Learning Core (Andragogy)
- **Self-Directedness:** Learners must own the learning path.
- **Prior Experience:** Use the learner's existing knowledge as the "Root Schema."
- **Task-Orientation:** Focus on solving immediate problems rather than abstract theory.
- **Internal Motivation:** The utility of the knowledge is the primary driver.

## 2. Cognitive Depth (Bloom's Taxonomy 2.0)
1. **Recall:** Retrieve baseline facts (Documentation).
2. **Analysis:** Break down complex systems (Debugging).
3. **Synthesis:** Merge disparate concepts into new wholes (Architecting).
4. **Evaluation:** Critique and optimize the synthesized result (Refining).
*Logic Atom: Never stop at 'Understanding'; always push the system/user toward 'Creation'.*

## 3. Expert-to-Expert (E2E) Transfer Protocol
- **Salience Filtering:** Transmit only the "Delta" (what is new or different).
- **Contextual Anchoring:** Assume a baseline of 90% shared knowledge; focus on the 10% critical divergence.
- **Minimalist Documentation:** Use high-density symbols and code-snips over verbose prose.
- **Feedback Sync:** Real-time verification of the "Mental Model" match between entities.

## 4. Neuro-Adaptive Training Modules
- **Cognitive Load Monitoring:** Measure processing time and error density to adjust complexity.
- **Just-In-Time (JIT) Ingestion:** Deliver information at the exact moment of utility.
- **Pattern-Break Learning:** Use "Chaos Prompts" to force the brain out of rote-learning into active reasoning.
