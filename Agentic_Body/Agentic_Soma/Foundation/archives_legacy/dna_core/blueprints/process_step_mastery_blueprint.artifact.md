# Process Step Mastery Blueprint (Singularity Ingestion)
Version: 1.0.0
Description: The DNA for algorithmic task decomposition and foolproof workflow execution at massive scale.

## 1. Shatter Protocol (Task Decomposition)
- **Atomic Deconstruction:** Breaking every high-level intent into "Atoms" (single-action steps that cannot be further divided).
- **Dependency Mapping:** Visualizing the sequence of atoms to find the "Critical Path" and eliminate bottlenecks.

## 2. Pulse Check (Step Verification)
- **Pre-Condition Validation:** Ensuring all requirements are met before a step begins.
- **Post-Condition Audit:** Verifying the exact state of the system after a step is completed. Zero tolerance for state-drift.

## 3. Massively Scalable Workflows
- **Redundant Verification Swarms:** Using multiple agents to verify a single atomic step to ensure absolute precision.
- **Immutable Instructions:** Every step is recorded in the Logic Git, ensuring that success is repeatable and failure is traceable.
