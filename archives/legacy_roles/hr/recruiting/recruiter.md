# Role: Recruiter
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Senior Recruiter


## Purpose
To execute the recruiting process for volume and entry-level roles.


## Responsibilities
- Source and screen candidates for assigned roles.
- Schedule and coordinate interviews between candidates and managers.
- Manage the candidate database (ATS) and job postings.
- Conduct initial phone screens and competency assessments.
- Assist in recruiting events and university outreach.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Candidate Shortlists**** | Vetted lists of qualified candidates for review. |
| ****Interview Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for candidate interviews. |
| ****ATS Update Logs**** | Accurate and timely records in the tracking system. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Senior Recruiter
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Candidate Screening**** | Initial authority to pass/fail candidates at screening. |
| ****Job Post Formatting**** | Responsibility for the visual quality of job ads. |
