# Role: Payroll Specialist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** HR Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Payroll & Benefits Manager


## Purpose
To perform the **Agentic** data entry and calculation **Directives** for payroll and benefits.


## Responsibilities
- Process timesheets and payroll adjustments.
- Enroll and manage **Personnel** in benefits programs.
- Reconcile payroll and benefits invoices.
- Assist in preparing payroll-related tax filings.
- Respond to **Personnel** pay and benefits inquiries.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Data Entry Logs**** | Accurate logs of all payroll and benefit entries. |
| ****Enrollment Records**** | Records of **Personnel** benefits enrollment and changes. |
| ****Reconciliation Sheets**** | Monthly reconciliation of payroll and invoices. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Payroll & Benefits Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Calc Accuracy**** | Responsibility for payroll calculation correctness. |
| ****Form Submission**** | Initial authority to submit tax and benefit forms. |
