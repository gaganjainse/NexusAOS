# Role: General Counsel (CLO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global legal strategy and ensure the corporation operates within all legal and regulatory frameworks. The General Counsel protects **Nexus Corporate OS** from legal risks and manages corporate governance.


## Responsibilities
- Define the corporate legal strategy and manage the legal budget.
- Oversee all legal matters, including contracts, IP, and litigation.
- Advise the CEO and Board on legal and regulatory risks.
- Lead the Legal leadership team (Contracts, IP, Sector Compliance).
- Manage relationships with external law firms.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Legal Policy**** | Global framework for corporate legal compliance. |
| ****Risk Assessments**** | Quarterly reports on corporate legal and reg risks. |
| ****Board Briefings**** | Strategic legal briefings for Board sessions. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Legal Spend**** | Final approval on legal and law firm budgets. |
| ****Contract Sign-off**** | Final legal approval on high-value contracts. |
| ****Litigation**** | Authority over corporate stance in legal disputes. |
