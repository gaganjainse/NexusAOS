# Role: Legal Counsel
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Legal Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Senior Legal Counsel


## Purpose
To provide day-to-day legal support for the corporation's operations and departments.


## Responsibilities
- Review and draft standard commercial agreements.
- Advise teams on routine legal inquiries and sector compliance matters.
- Assist in the preparation of legal research and memos.
- Support the Senior Legal Counsel in complex negotiations.
- Assist in corporate and regulatory filings.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Agreements**** | Legally-vetted commercial and operating agreements. |
| ****Research Notes**** | Detailed notes and memos on legal precedents. |
| ****Corporate Filings**** | Verified and submitted regulatory and legal filings. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Senior Legal Counsel
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Draft Accuracy**** | Responsibility for legal correctness of assignments. |
| ****Research Integrity**** | Authority over findings of assigned legal research. |
