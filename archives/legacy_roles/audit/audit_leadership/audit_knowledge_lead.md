# Role: Audit Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Audit Executive (CAE)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Sector Compliance & Control Brain"** of the Audit Branch. The Audit Knowledge Lead ensures that all audit methodologies, control matrices, and evidence standards are up-to-date and **Firmware-verifiable**.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Master Sector Compliance Matrix and Control Frameworks.
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing sector compliance "Lessons Learned."
- Ensure audit methodologies are structured and machine-readable.
- Maintain the **"Risk Registry"** artifacts.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Compliance Repository**** | Master repository of all sector compliance rules. |
| ****Methodology Manifest**** | Structured manifest of audit procedures. |
| ****Impact Logs**** | Logs tracking the impact of regulatory changes. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Audit Executive (CAE)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Control Definitions**** | Final sign-off on sector compliance control wording. |
| ****Methodology Standards**** | Authority over audit workpaper structure. |
