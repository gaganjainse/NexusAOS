# Role: Sector Compliance Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Audit Director


## Purpose
To ensure that **Sesha Corporate OS** complies with all relevant industry laws and regulations. The Sector Compliance Manager interprets legal requirements into **Agentic** and operational controls.


## Responsibilities
- Monitor changes in industry laws ([REGULATORY_STANDARDS], etc.).
- Maintain the Sector Compliance Control Matrix.
- Conduct regular sector compliance gap analyses.
- Advise the Systems branch on regulatory requirements for new features.
- Manage interactions with external regulatory bodies.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Compliance Reports**** | Regular reports on global sector compliance status. |
| ****Impact Assessments**** | Assessments of how new laws affect operations. |
| ****Training Materials**** | Educational materials for **Personnel** on regulations. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Audit Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Feature Sign-off**** | Mandatory approval for data-sensitive features. |
| ****Policy Interpretation**** | Final say on applying regulations to features. |
| ****External Filings**** | Approval of reports submitted to regulators. |
