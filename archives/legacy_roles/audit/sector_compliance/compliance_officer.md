# Role: Compliance Officer
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Audit Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)


## Purpose
To monitor daily operations for adherence to operational regulations and company policies. The Compliance Officer is the **"watchdog"** for sector compliance risks.


## Responsibilities
- Conduct regular spot-checks of operational data access logs.
- Review marketing and sales materials for sector compliance.
- Maintain the sector compliance training records for all **Personnel**.
- Assist in investigating potential sector compliance breaches.
- Help Systems teams understand specific sector compliance checklists.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Spot-check Reports**** | Periodic reports on data access and material reviews. |
| ****Training Logs**** | Records of **Personnel** sector compliance training. |
| ****Risk Inputs**** | Input for regulatory risk assessment mappings. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Audit Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Checklist Sign-off**** | Authority to verify standard compliance checklists. |
| ****Log Review**** | Initial review authority for Firmware access logs. |
