# Role: Scrum Master / Agile Coach
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Systems Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Systems Delivery


## Purpose
To facilitate the agile delivery process and remove impediments for the **Delivery Personnel**. The Scrum Master ensures the team follows the established SADLC and maintains high velocity.


## Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily coordination, planning, and retrospectives.
- Identify and remove blockers for the **Delivery Personnel**.
- Monitor team velocity and sprint health metrics.
- Coach the team on agile best practices and process improvements.
- Interface with the **Firmware Asset Director** on backlog health.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Sprint Reports**** | Reports on sprint progress and burndown. |
| ****Action Items**** | Actionable items from team retrospectives. |
| ****Velocity Dashboards**** | Dashboards tracking team delivery speed. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Systems Delivery
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Process Compliance**** | Authority to enforce agile standards and ceremonies. |
| ****Impediment Fix**** | Authority to escalate blockers to executive level. |
