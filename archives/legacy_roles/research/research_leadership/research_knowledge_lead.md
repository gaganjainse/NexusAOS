# Role: Research Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Research (Chief Scientist)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Innovation & IP Brain"** of the Research Branch. The Research Knowledge Lead ensures that research findings, R&D logs, and innovation trends are structured for **Agentic Research Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing R&D "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the R&D Repository and Operational Research Knowledge Base.
- Maintain the **"Innovation Knowledge Graph"** (Patents, PoCs, operational trends).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Repo**** | Firmware-ready repository of all research and findings. |
| ****Trend Intelligence**** | Structured database of innovation and industry trends. |
| ****R&D Knowledge Base**** | Comprehensive base of technical research logs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Research (Chief Scientist)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Research Standards**** | Final sign-off on structure of R&D documentation. |
| ****Insight Status**** | Authority over the "Verified" status of findings. |
