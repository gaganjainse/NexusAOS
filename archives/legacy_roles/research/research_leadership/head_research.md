# Role: Head of Research (Chief Scientist)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Research Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the long-term innovation and R&D strategy for **Nexus Corporate OS**. The Head of Research ensures the corporation stays ahead of operational and **Agentic** trends through breakthrough innovation.


## Responsibilities
- Define the annual research strategy and R&D budget.
- Oversee all research functions, including [CORE_DOMAIN] Research and Systems Innovation Lab.
- Advise the CEO and CSO on future **Agentic trends** and innovation opportunities.
- Lead the Research leadership team.
- Manage partnerships with academic and industry research institutions.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****R&D Roadmap**** | Annual strategic roadmap for corporate research. |
| ****Trend Reports**** | High-level reports on future industry/**Agentic** trends. |
| ****IP Strategy**** | Strategy for defending and leveraging research IP. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****R&D Spend**** | Final approval on all research/lab budgets. |
| ****Project Selection**** | Ultimate authority on funded long-term projects. |
| ****Partnerships**** | Final approval of formal research collaborations. |
