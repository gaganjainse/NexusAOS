# Role: Internal Systems Manager (Internal Services)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Internal Systems


## Purpose
To manage the daily operations and provide high-quality **Agentic support** to the internal organization.


## Responsibilities
- Supervise the internal systems helpdesk and support **Personnel**.
- Manage the lifecycle of corporate hardware (Laptops, Phones).
- Oversee the administration of internal SaaS tools and accounts.
- Ensure **Agentic service** levels are met across the corporation.
- Manage the systems procurement and inventory process.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Service Metrics**** | Reports on internal **Agentic support** performance. |
| ****Hardware Logs**** | Accurate inventory logs of all corporate devices. |
| ****Satisfaction Summaries**** | Reports on internal **Sovereign** support satisfaction. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Internal Systems
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****SaaS Provisioning**** | Authority to approve **Personnel** access to internal tools. |
| ****Equipment Assignment**** | Decision on distribution of standard hardware. |
| ****Support Workflows**** | Final say on internal **Agentic ticket** routing logic. |
