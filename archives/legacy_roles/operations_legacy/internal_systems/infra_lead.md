# Role: Internal Infrastructure Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Internal Systems


## Purpose
To lead the design and maintenance of the internal corporate network and server **Agentic infrastructure**.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the internal networking and **Agentic engineering** team.
- Design and manage the corporate VPN and internal security perimeters.
- Oversee the maintenance of internal servers and storage.
- Manage the corporate internet and connectivity services.
- Ensure the reliability of internal communication tools (Video conferencing, VoIP).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Network Architecture**** | Strategic designs for internal corporate networking. |
| ****Uptime Reports**** | Reports on availability of internal **Agentic infrastructure**. |
| ****Security Audit Logs**** | Documentation of internal network security events. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Internal Systems
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Network Changes**** | Final sign-off on internal network configurations. |
| ****Security Protocols**** | Authority over internal firewall and access rules. |
| ****Hardware Selection**** | Decision on internal server and network hardware. |
