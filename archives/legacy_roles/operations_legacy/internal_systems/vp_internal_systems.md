# Role: VP of Internal Systems
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)


## Purpose
To lead the internal **Agentic strategy** and ensure the reliability, security, and scalability of the corporation's digital tools and systems.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the internal systems, Networking, and Infrastructure teams.
- Oversee corporate-wide hardware and software procurement.
- Ensure the security and availability of internal **Agentic infrastructure** and data.
- Drive **Agentic service management** (ITSM) excellence.
- Manage relationships with internal **Agentic** and SaaS vendors.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Systems Roadmap**** | Annual strategy for internal corporate **Agentic systems**. |
| ****Compliance Audits**** | Internal reports on **Agentic security** and compliance. |
| ****Reliability Reports**** | Dashboards tracking internal infrastructure uptime. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Operating Officer (COO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Systems Standards**** | Final say on corporate hardware/software standards. |
| ****SaaS Selection**** | Approval of all internal software and SaaS tools. |
| ****Network Arch**** | Final approval on internal network designs. |
