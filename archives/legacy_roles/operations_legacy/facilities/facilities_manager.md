# Role: Facilities Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Operations Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Workplace & Facilities


## Purpose
To manage the daily operations and maintenance of the corporate physical offices.


## Responsibilities
- Supervise the maintenance, janitorial, and security services.
- Oversee facility repairs and upgrade projects.
- Manage facility-related vendor contracts and performance.
- Ensure the physical safety and accessibility of the workplace.
- Manage the facility budget and expense tracking.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Maintenance Logs**** | Verified records of completed facility repairs. |
| ****Safety Audits**** | Regular reports on workplace safety and compliance. |
| ****Vendor Reviews**** | Performance reviews for facility service providers. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Workplace & Facilities
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Repair Spend**** | Authority to approve standard facility repairs. |
| ****Service Timing**** | Final say on scheduling of maintenance activity. |
| ****Safety Sign-off**** | Initial authority to mark projects as safe for use. |
