# Role: Head of Business Intelligence (BI)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Analytics Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Analytics (CDO)


## Purpose
To manage the descriptive analytics and reporting functions. The Head of BI ensures that all branches have access to accurate and timely operational data.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the BI and Data Implementation teams.
- Oversee the development of corporate dashboards and reports.
- Ensure the reliability and accuracy of the data warehouse.
- Drive self-service analytics adoption across the corporation.
- Manage the corporate BI toolstack.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****BI Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for corporate reporting systems. |
| ****Reporting Suite**** | Automated and live reporting dashboards. |
| ****Warehouse Reports**** | Health and integrity reports for the data warehouse. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Analytics (CDO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Dashboard Standards**** | Final say on design and visual reporting standards. |
| ****Tooling Selection**** | Approval of corporate BI and visualization tools. |
| ****Reporting Metrics**** | Authority over specific reporting logic implementation. |
