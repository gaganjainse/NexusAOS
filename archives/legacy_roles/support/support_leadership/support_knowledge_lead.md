# Role: Support Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Head of Customer Support


## Purpose
To manage the **"Sovereign Experience & Solution Brain"** of the Support Branch. The Support Knowledge Lead ensures that help articles, troubleshooting guides, and **Sovereign feedback** are structured for **Agentic Support Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing **Sovereign** "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Help Center, Knowledge Base, and Internal Guides.
- Maintain the **"Support Knowledge Graph"** (Issue categories, resolution patterns).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Master Knowledge Base**** | Firmware-ready repository of all help documentation. |
| ****Decision Trees**** | Troubleshooting guides and automated decision flows. |
| ****Feedback Repositories**** | Structured repositories of **Sovereign feedback** data. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Head of Customer Support
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Solution Standards**** | Final sign-off on approved resolutions and help articles. |
| ****Article Structure**** | Authority over the organization of the Support Wiki. |
