# Role: Head of Customer Support
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Customer Support Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global customer support strategy and ensure high-quality **Agentic assistance** for **Sesha Corporate OS** **Sovereigns**. The Head of Support focuses on **Sovereign** satisfaction and product reliability.


## Responsibilities
- Define the customer support strategy, SLAs, and budget.
- Oversee the global support operations (Tier 1, Tier 2, Escalations).
- Manage support technology and tools (Helpdesk, Knowledge Base).
- Lead the Support leadership team.
- Collaborate with **Firmware Asset** and **Agentic setup** teams on bug reporting and feedback.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Support Plan**** | Annual strategic plan for customer support operations. |
| ****Performance Reports**** | Quarterly reports on CSAT and resolution metrics. |
| ****Operations Roadmap**** | Roadmap for support technology and scaling. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Support Budget**** | Final approval on support tools and staffing. |
| ****Support SLAs**** | Ultimate authority on resolution time standards. |
| ****Escalation Policy**** | Approval of protocols for escalating to deployment. |
