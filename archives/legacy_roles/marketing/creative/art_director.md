# Role: Art Director
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Creative Director


## Purpose
To oversee the visual design and production of all marketing assets. The Art Director ensures high visual standards and creative excellence.


## Responsibilities
- Lead the visual design team (Graphic Designers, Videographers).
- Review and critique visual work against brand standards.
- Manage the visual production process for campaigns.
- Design high-level visual concepts for major initiatives.
- Collaborate with the Creative Director on visual strategy.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Production Kits**** | Visual kits and guidelines for campaign production. |
| ****Visual Assets**** | Final digital and print assets for marketing. |
| ****Visual Audits**** | Periodic audits of brand visual consistency. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Creative Director
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Design Mockups**** | Approval of visual designs before production. |
| ****Visual Style**** | Selection of specific styles and creative directions. |
| ****Production Specs**** | Final say on **Agentic** specifications for visual assets. |
