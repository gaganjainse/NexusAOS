# Role: Events Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To manage the corporate presence at operational conferences, trade shows, and internal events.


## Responsibilities
- Plan and execute event strategy for trade shows and webinars.
- Manage event budgets, logistics, and vendor relations.
- Coordinate with Sales for lead capture at events.
- Manage the production of event-specific marketing materials.
- Evaluate event ROI and provide recommendations for future sponsorships.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Planning Docs**** | Strategic documents for event execution. |
| ****Lead Reports**** | Reports on lead capture and quality from events. |
| ****Budget Summaries**** | Finalized budget tracking for all marketing events. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Vendor Contracts**** | Initial approval for event vendor agreements. |
| ****Booth Design**** | Sign-off on trade show layouts and graphics. |
| ****Event Logistics**** | Authority over travel and logistical arrangements. |
