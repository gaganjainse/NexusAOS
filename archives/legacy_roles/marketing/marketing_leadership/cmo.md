# Role: Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the global brand and growth strategy for **Nexus Corporate OS**. The CMO ensures that the corporation effectively communicates its value to the industry and scales its market presence.


## Responsibilities
- Define the global marketing strategy, brand identity, and budget.
- Oversee all marketing functions, including Content, Creative, Events, and Performance.
- Advise the CEO on market trends, competitive positioning, and growth opportunities.
- Lead the Marketing leadership team.
- Manage major brand partnerships and PR strategy.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Marketing Roadmap**** | Annual global strategy and budget for marketing. |
| ****Brand Guidelines**** | Master guidelines for brand voice and identity. |
| ****Growth Reports**** | Monthly reports on global growth and sentiment. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Marketing Spend**** | Final approval on all marketing and event budgets. |
| ****Brand Voice**** | Ultimate authority on public messaging and identity. |
| ****Campaign Launch**** | Final sign-off on major global campaigns. |
