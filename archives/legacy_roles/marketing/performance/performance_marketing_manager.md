# Role: Performance Marketing Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Marketing Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Marketing


## Purpose
To drive user acquisition and growth through data-driven digital marketing channels.


## Responsibilities
- Manage paid search (SEM), paid social, and display advertising.
- Optimize conversion rates across the marketing funnel.
- Conduct A/B testing on landing pages and ad creative.
- Track and report on CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) and LTV.
- Manage digital marketing agency relationships.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Media Dashboards**** | Live dashboards tracking performance marketing ROI. |
| ****CRO Reports**** | Analysis of conversion rate optimization tests. |
| ****Spend Breakdowns**** | Detailed reports on digital advertising expenditure. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Marketing
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Ad Spend**** | Authority over daily and weekly digital budgets. |
| ****Keyword Strategy**** | Final say on targeted search keyword lists. |
| ****Landing Pages**** | Joint approval on conversion-focused layouts. |
