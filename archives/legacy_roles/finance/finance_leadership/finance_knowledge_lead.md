# Role: Finance Knowledge Lead (Branch HQ)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch (HQ Layer)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)


## Purpose
To manage the **"Fiscal & Economic Brain"** of the Finance Branch. The Finance Knowledge Lead ensures that budgeting rules, tax logic, and financial policies are structured for **Agentic Finance Firmware**.


## Responsibilities
- Serve as the Branch Learning Coordinator, capturing fiscal "Lessons Learned."
- Manage the Financial Policy and Budgeting standards repository.
- Maintain the **"Fiscal Knowledge Graph"** (Cost centers, budget owners).


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Policy Repository**** | Firmware-ready repository of all fiscal policies. |
| ****Budgeting Standards**** | Standardized rules for forecasting and budgeting. |
| ****Logic Artifacts**** | Artifacts defining corporate financial logic. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Fiscal Rules**** | Final sign-off on financial rule wording. |
| ****Model Logic**** | Authority over assumptions in financial artifacts. |
