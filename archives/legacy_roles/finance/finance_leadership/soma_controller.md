# Role: Corporate Controller
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)


## Purpose
To lead the corporate accounting function and ensure accurate financial reporting and internal controls.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the accounting team and the month-end close process.
- Oversee financial statement preparation according to [FINANCIAL_REPORTING_STANDARDS].
- Ensure corporate-wide accounting compliance and policy management.
- Manage external financial audits.
- Oversee accounts payable, accounts receivable, and payroll accounting.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Financial Statements**** | Finalized and verified corporate statements. |
| ****Control Docs**** | Documentation of internal financial controls. |
| ****Audit Packages**** | Readiness packages for external financial audits. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Accounting Policy**** | Final say on the corporate accounting manual. |
| ****Journal Entries**** | Authority to approve high-value manual entries. |
| ****Financial Systems**** | Selection of accounting and ERP software. |
