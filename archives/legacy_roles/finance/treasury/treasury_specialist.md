# Role: Treasury Specialist
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Finance Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Treasury Manager


## Purpose
To support the treasury function with cash monitoring and administrative **Directives**.


## Responsibilities
- Monitor daily bank balances and report on cash levels.
- Initiate bank transfers and payments under supervision.
- Manage bank account documentation and authorized signer records.
- Assist in the preparation of cash flow forecasts.
- Support corporate insurance and credit management.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Activity Reports**** | Daily logs of corporate banking activity. |
| ****Forecast Inputs**** | Structured inputs for cash flow forecasting. |
| ****Account Logs**** | Real-time status logs for all bank accounts. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Treasury Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Data Entry**** | Responsibility for accuracy of cash reporting. |
| ****Coordination**** | Authority over organization of banking docs. |
