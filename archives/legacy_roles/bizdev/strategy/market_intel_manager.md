# Role: Market Intelligence Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Strategy


## Purpose
To lead the gathering and analysis of market, competitor, and industry data.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the market intelligence and industry research team.
- Oversee the competitor monitoring and win/loss analysis process.
- Drive the internal distribution of market and competitor insights.
- Manage external market research and data vendor relationships.
- Coordinate with Sales on competitive battlecards and enablement.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Insight Reports**** | Weekly reports on market trends and insights. |
| ****Competitor Battlecards**** | Structured battlecards for sales enablement. |
| ****Research Summaries**** | Comprehensive summaries of market research. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Strategy
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Intel Accuracy**** | Responsibility for the integrity of competitor data. |
| ****Vendor Selection**** | Initial approval for market research providers. |
