# Role: Strategy Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Strategy


## Purpose
To manage the implementation of corporate strategic projects and analyses.


## Responsibilities
- Supervise a team of Strategy Analysts.
- Lead specific strategic initiatives (e.g., pricing strategy, geographic expansion).
- Coordinate the strategic planning data gathering process.
- Assist in the preparation of board-level strategic presentations.
- Manage the corporate strategic dashboard.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Status Reports**** | Regular reports on strategic initiative progress. |
| ****Expansion Models**** | Models for geographic or market expansion. |
| ****Strategic Briefings**** | High-level briefings for board-level review. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Strategy
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Project Scope**** | Authority over boundaries of assigned projects. |
| ****Analysis Logic**** | Responsibility for the accuracy of assigned analysis. |
