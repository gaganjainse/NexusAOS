# Role: Market Intelligence Analyst
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Market Intelligence Manager


## Purpose
To gather and analyze data on competitors, markets, and operational trends.


## Responsibilities
- Monitor competitor product launches, pricing, and news.
- Analyze market data and operational industry reports.
- Support the creation of competitor battlecards and briefings.
- Conduct primary and secondary market research.
- Assist in win/loss analysis data collection.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Competitor Briefs**** | Detailed profiles and briefings on key competitors. |
| ****Market Data Sheets**** | Structured data sheets from market research. |
| ****Win/Loss Summaries**** | Summaries of win/loss analysis data. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Market Intelligence Manager
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Data Integrity**** | Responsibility for the accuracy of research data. |
