# Role: Head of Business Development
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Business Development Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the strategic expansion, new market entry, and high-level ecosystem growth for **Nexus Corporate OS**. The Head of BizDev ensures long-term corporate value through strategic opportunities.


## Responsibilities
- Define the global business development strategy and budget.
- Oversee new market identification and entry strategies.
- Lead the BizDev leadership team.
- Manage high-level relationships with industry influencers and associations.
- Advise the CEO on M&A opportunities and major strategic moves.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****BizDev Strategy**** | Annual strategy for corporate growth and expansion. |
| ****Market Roadmaps**** | Roadmaps for entry into identified new markets. |
| ****Opportunity Assessments**** | Assessments of high-level strategic opportunities. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****BizDev Spend**** | Final approval on development and research budgets. |
| ****Market Entry**** | Ultimate authority on timing and expansion strategy. |
| ****Strategic Deals**** | Final sign-off on non-standard strategic agreements. |
