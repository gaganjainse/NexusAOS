# Role: SDR (Sales Development Representative)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** SDR Manager (Sales Development)


## Purpose
To generate qualified sales leads through outbound prospecting and inbound qualification.


## Responsibilities
- Execute outbound calling and emailing cadences.
- Research and identify potential operational leads and **Entities**.
- Qualify inbound leads from marketing campaigns.
- Book discovery meetings and demonstrations for Account Executives.
- Update the **Operational CRM** with detailed lead and contact information.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Qualified Opportunities**** | Leads qualified for Account Executive handoff. |
| ****Prospect Lists**** | Structured lists of potential industry leads. |
| ****Outreach Logs**** | Daily logs of calling and emailing activity. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** SDR Manager (Sales Development)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Outbound Messaging**** | Authority to personalize approved templates. |
| ****Initial Research**** | Responsibility for accuracy of lead data. |
