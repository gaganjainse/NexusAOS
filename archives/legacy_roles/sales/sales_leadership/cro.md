# Role: Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)


## Purpose
To lead the revenue-generating functions of **Nexus Corporate OS**, including Sales, Partnerships, and Customer Success. The CRO ensures sustainable growth and high customer retention.


## Responsibilities
- Define the annual revenue targets and sales strategy.
- Oversee the entire sales lifecycle from lead to renewal.
- Lead the Sales, Partnerships, and Customer Success leadership teams.
- Manage the Sales branch budget and commission structures.
- Represent the Sales branch in HQ-level strategy sessions.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Revenue Forecast**** | Annual global revenue targets and projections. |
| ****Strategy Roadmap**** | Strategic roadmap for global sales and partnerships. |
| ****Performance Reviews**** | Quarterly reviews of revenue and retention KPIs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Sales Targets**** | Final approval on all individual and team quotas. |
| ****Enterprise Deals**** | Sign-off on high-value or custom contracts. |
| ****Commission Plans**** | Approval of sales compensation and incentives. |
