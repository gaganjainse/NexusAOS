# Role: SME Sales Manager
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Sales


## Purpose
To lead the sales efforts targeting small and medium-sized core business units.


## Responsibilities
- Manage a team of SME Account Executives.
- Drive high-volume sales productivity and efficiency.
- Monitor SME pipeline health and sales velocity.
- Implement sales scripts and playbooks for shorter sales cycles.
- Manage lead distribution and conversion rates for the SME segment.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Performance Dashboard**** | Dashboards tracking SME sales metrics. |
| ****High-volume Playbooks**** | Optimized scripts for shorter SME sales cycles. |
| ****Conversion Reports**** | Weekly reports on segment-specific conversion rates. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Sales
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****SME Discounts**** | Authority over segment-specific price adjustments. |
| ****Playbook Updates**** | Final say on SME-targeted sales messaging. |
