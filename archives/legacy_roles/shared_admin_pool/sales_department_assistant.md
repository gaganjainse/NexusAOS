# Role: Sales Department Assistant
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Sales Branch
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** VP of Sales


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Sales Branch leadership and teams.


## Responsibilities
- Schedule sales team coordination and pipeline reviews.
- Manage **Agentic tool** licenses and CRM access requests.
- Coordinate sales department travel and client meeting logistics.
- Assist in the organization of sales training and **Sovereign** events.
- Maintain the sales branch internal directory and playbook logs.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Leadership Schedules**** | Coordinated calendars for sales branch leadership. |
| ****Expense Logs**** | Documentation of department and license spend. |
| ****Meeting Minutes**** | Records of pipeline reviews and strategy syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** VP of Sales
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Scheduling**** | Authority over sales leadership meeting availability. |
| ****Tooling Provision**** | Initial review of team-level CRM access requests. |
