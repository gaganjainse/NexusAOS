# Role: Analytics Department Firmware
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Analytics Branch
**Level:** Support Layer
**Superior:** Head of Analytics (CDO)


## Purpose
To provide administrative and logistical support to the Analytics Branch leadership.


## Responsibilities
- Manage the Head of Analytics' calendar and meetings.
- Handle data-related vendor invoices and licensing.
- Coordinate analytics department travel and events.
- Assist in the organization of internal data training and coordination.
- Maintain the analytics branch internal directory and wiki.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Leadership Schedules** | Coordinated calendars for analytics leadership. |
| **Expense Logs** | Documentation of department equipment and licenses. |
| **Meeting Minutes** | Records of data strategy and analytics syncs. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** [Head of Analytics](../analytics/leadership/head_analytics.md)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scheduling** | Authority over analytics leadership availability. |
| **Supply Requests** | Initial review of equipment and licensing needs. |
