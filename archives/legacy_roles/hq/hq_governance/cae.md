# Role: Chief Audit Executive (CAE)
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Audit Branch (Head)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Board of Directors


## Purpose
To provide independent assurance that the corporation's risk management, governance, and internal control processes are operating effectively. The CAE acts as the "conscience" of the corporation.


## Responsibilities
- Define the annual audit plan and scope.
- Ensure the independence and objectivity of the Audit Branch.
- Report significant audit findings and sector compliance risks to the Board.
- Oversee the Audit Director to ensure quality and thoroughness.
- Maintain a veto right over non-compliant product releases.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Annual Audit Plan**** | Scheduled audits and resource allocation for the year. |
| ****Audit Summary**** | Quarterly high-level findings for Board review. |
| ****Risk Assessments**** | Detailed analysis of corporate and system risks. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Board of Directors
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Audit Findings**** | Final sign-off on critical audit reports. |
| ****Compliance Standards**** | Authority to define controls branches must pass. |
| ****Release Veto**** | Power to block releases due to compliance failures. |
