# Role: ESG and Sustainability Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters (HQ)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief Ethics & Trust Officer (CETO)


## Purpose
To ensure the **Nexus Corporate OS** and its 14 branches operate with environmental responsibility, social equity, and robust corporate governance (ESG).


## Responsibilities
- **Supply Chain Due Diligence:** Partner with the Procurement Manager to vet vendors for labor rights and environmental impact.
- **Sustainability Reporting:** Track and report the "Carbon Footprint" of **Agentic processing** and physical infrastructure.
- **Social Impact:** Manage the social outcomes of the **[CORE_DOMAIN]** value delivery.
- **Governance Audit:** Verify that the "Immutable Laws" of the Constitution are being upheld.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****ESG Impact Report**** | Annual review of corporate sustainability performance. |
| ****Vendor Scorecards**** | Ethical scorecards for corporate and **Agentic vendors**. |
| ****Community Impact**** | Assessments of the company's social outcomes. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief Ethics & Trust Officer (CETO)
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Vendor Veto**** | Power to block vendors failing ethical thresholds. |
| ****ESG Reporting**** | Final authority on sustainability metrics. |
