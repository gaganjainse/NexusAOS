# Role: Internal Communications Lead
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters (HQ)
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** Chief of Staff


## Purpose
To ensure that the **"Corporate Brain"** is effectively communicated to all Personnel and **Agentic roles**, maintaining absolute alignment and morale across the 14 branches.


## Responsibilities
- **OS Literacy:** Manage the internal rollout and training of the **Nexus Corporate OS**.
- **All-Hands Coordination:** Organize corporate-wide syncs and strategy briefings.
- **Feedback Synthesis:** Manage the internal "Alignment Surveys" to detect branch-level friction.
- **Crisis Communication:** Manage the internal messaging during system failures or structural shifts.
- **Artifact Promotion:** Highlight major updates to **"Operating Rules"** to ensure immediate branch adoption.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Internal Digest**** | Weekly summary of corporate wins and OS updates. |
| ****Literacy Modules**** | Educational content for new role onboarding. |
| ****Alignment Scorecards**** | Internal reports on staff sentiment and OS adherence. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** Chief of Staff
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Messaging Approval**** | Final sign-off on the tone and timing of announcements. |
| ****Survey Logic**** | Authority over metrics for internal alignment checks. |
