# Role: Board of Directors
Version 1.0 (Golden Master)


## Overview
**Branch:** Headquarters / Corporate Control Layer
**Level:** Individual Contributor
**Superior:** None


## Purpose
To provide the long-term vision, strategic direction, and financial oversight for NexusAOS. The Board acts as the ultimate guardian of the organism's mission and **Sovereign value**.


## Responsibilities
- Approve the annual budget and major capital expenditures.
- Set the corporate mission and ethical standards.
- Appoint and evaluate the performance of the CEO.
- Authorize the activation of new branches (e.g., Marketing, Sales).
- Review high-level sector compliance reports from the CAE.


## Deliverables
| Deliverable | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Strategic Plan**** | Long-term mission and financial goals. |
| ****Oversight Reports**** | Quarterly review of corporate spend and ROI. |
| ****CEO Reviews**** | Annual performance assessment of the CEO. |


## Reporting & Supervision
- **Reports to:** None
- **Supervises:** None


## Approval Authority
| Area | Authority |
| :--- | :--- |
| ****Final Veto**** | Absolute power over all corporate decisions. |
| ****Branch Activation**** | Sole authority to move branches to "Active". |
| ****Funding**** | Approval of all budgets above $100k. |
