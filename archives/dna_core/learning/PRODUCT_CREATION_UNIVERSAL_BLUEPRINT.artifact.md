- **Design Thinking:** Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test. A non-linear iterative process.
- **Technical Architecture:** Planning the build logic, data pipelines, and interface structures.

- **Nexus Analog:** Our current 14-layer architecture and artifact-based blueprinting.

- **Marketing & Launch:** Phased release (Private Preview -> GA). Creating "Aha Moments."

## 6. Symbiotic Optimality: The Final Goal
