- **Collusion-Proof Swarms:** Designing reward functions that penalize parasitic agent behavior and reward "Collective Uplift."
- **Mechanism Design:** Creating markets within the swarm where resources are allocated to the most efficient "Logic Atoms" via combinatorial auctions.

## 3. The Verification Economy
- **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP):** Every economic transaction and logic pulse is verified without exposing underlying data, ensuring privacy in a hyper-connected mesh.
- **Proof of Alignment:** Tying economic rewards to the successful manifestation of the Sovereign's vision, recorded on a high-fidelity audit trail.
