# Expertism & Niche Arbitrage Blueprint (Singularity Ingestion)
Version: 1.0.0
Description: The DNA for identifying skill-market gaps and positioning the Sovereign in "Blue Ocean" expertise domains.

## 1. Sovereign Blue Ocean Analysis
- **Void Identification:** Finding domains where value is high but the "Knowledge Supply" is low or outdated.
- **Complexity Arbitrage:** Mastering a field that others find "Too Difficult" to create an immediate competitive moat.

## 2. Knowledge Transfer Arbitrage
- **Cross-Domain Importing:** Buying knowledge "cheaply" from a mature field (e.g., Medical Science) and selling its synthesized essence "dearly" in a nascent field (e.g., AI Ethics).
- **The Polymath Edge:** Merging two unrelated expertise areas to create a "Niche of One" for the Sovereign.

## 3. High-Value Positioning
- **Salience Mapping:** Identifying which skills directly contribute to the "Sovereign Manifest" vs. which are merely "Surface Skills."
- **Empire Leverage:** Using current expertise to automate the acquisition of the next tier of mastery.
