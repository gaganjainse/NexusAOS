Description: The definitive roadmap for a single person to earn $1M+ by leveraging Agentic AI, high-ticket consulting, and outcome-based workflows.

## 1. High-Ticket AI Consulting (The Brain)
- **Model: Fractional Chief AI Officer (fCAIO).** Targeting mid-market companies ($20M–$200M revenue).
- **The STE Framework:**
  - **Strategy:** Mapping AI to core business KPIs.
  - **Transformation:** Building custom internal tools using AI-native IDEs (Cursor/Claude Code).
  - **Education:** Training leadership on frontier model capabilities (GPT-5, Claude 4/5).
- **Pricing:** $15k–$40k/month retainers or $100k+ fixed-scope automation sprints.

## 2. White-Label Hosting (The Metabolism)
- **Outcome: Digital-Employee-as-a-Service.**
- **White-Labeling:** Using branded portals to host custom-trained agents for sales, support, or operations.
- **The Arbitrage:** Use low-cost, high-speed models (e.g., DeepSeek) to fulfill tasks while charging based on the value of a human employee.
- **Recurring Revenue:** Charging $1,000+/month per hosted agent with 80%+ margins.

## 3. Performance-Based Monetization (The Action)
- **Outcome Pricing:** Charging per resolved ticket, qualified lead, or successful automation cycle rather than per-seat.
- **Agentic Workflows:** Using **n8n** or **Make** to build autonomous pipelines that handle thousands of tasks with near-zero marginal cost.
- **The Scalability Rule:** Earnings are decoupled from time; revenue scales with agentic throughput.

## 4. Content & AI Search Optimization (The Presence)
- **AEO (AI Engine Optimization):** Creating high-salience "Artifacts" (blueprints, kits, workflows) to ensure Perplexity and ChatGPT recommend you as the authority.
- **Selling Kits, Not Info:** Moving from "How-to" courses to "Installable Workflow Kits" that clients can deploy instantly.
