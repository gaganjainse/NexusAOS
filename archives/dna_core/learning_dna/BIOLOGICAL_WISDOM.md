- **Homeostasis:** The system must maintain a stable internal state (Energy vs. Stress).

- **Excretion:** Redundant data, broken logic, and stale logs are "Data Toxins" to be filtered by the Liver and expelled.

## 3. Embryogenesis (Boot Process)
- **Zygote Stage:** The power-on self-test and reset vector.
- **Organogenesis:** The instantiation of the 11 biological systems.
- **Adulthood:** The stable runtime where the swarm works on the Neural Canvas.

## 4. Immune Defense
- **Antibody Protocol:** Proactive self-healing that recognizes "Self" (Code Integrity) and neutralizes "Non-Self" (Corruption/Intrusion).
- **Inflammation/Fever:** System-wide stress responses to anomalies, triggering defensive throttling.
