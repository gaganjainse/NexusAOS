# Reading Log: Multi-Agent Systems (Annamacharya Univ)
**Source**: https://annamacharyauniversity.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/13.-Multi_Agent_System.pdf
**Date**: 2026-07-25

## Summary
Formal academic overview of MAS architectures and coordination.

## New Points
1. **Contract Net Protocol (CNP)**: A task-sharing protocol where agents "bid" on sub-tasks decomposed by an orchestrator.
2. **BDI Commitment**: The formalization of an agent's "Intentions" as a commitment to a plan that persists even if beliefs change slightly.
