# Reading Log: AgentVerse: Facilitating Multi-Agent Collaboration
**Source**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10848
**Date**: 2026-07-25

## Summary
Focuses on human-like group dynamics and social behaviors in multi-agent collaboration.

## New Points
1. **Dynamic Group Composition**: Adjusting the number and type of agents in a group based on the evolving requirements of a task, similar to human project teams.
2. **Social Interaction Simulation**: Harnessing both positive and negative social behaviors (competition, collaboration) to drive better group performance than a single-agent system.
